CANDIDATE: crossover of the two given programs plus one new mechanism on the target axis.

WHAT I KEPT FROM EACH PROGRAM

From the parent I keep the arch, r22_retrieval_composition_renderer: the r18 path-state delta-rule
memory plus the capped nonlinear composition renderer over the retrieved-content channels. That is
the half that can actually carry a particular file's embedding through a chain of mv commands and
emit it at a read.

From the crossover partner I keep the objective, r22_exact_target_equivalence_quotient. This is not
a cosmetic swap, and the reason is visible in evolve/jail_data/train_sample.jsonl. render_obs()
builds the observation text as "cwd=... exit=...\n<output>", so:

  - every silent step (each `mv A B`, each `cat X > Y` redirect) renders to the byte-identical
    text "cwd=/ exit=0\n" and therefore to the byte-identical target embedding. In the sampled
    trajectory 6 of 25 steps are silent; across a batch of whole trajectories a large fraction of
    all target rows are literally the same vector.
  - the cups_test read step's observation is byte-identical to the observation of the expose step
    that first showed that content (in the sample: `cat /tmp/w/cups/usr/share/misc/magic.txt.1`
    returns exactly the text that `cat /tmp/w/cups/g53/f149.tmp` returned earlier).

The parent's objective, r12_antiretrieval_ring_negatives, treats every non-diagonal in-batch row as
a negative. So on this data it spends much of its gradient pushing the read step's prediction AWAY
from the expose step's target -- which is the correct answer -- and pushing every silent step away
from every other silent step's identical target. The quotient objective groups numerically equal
targets into one multi-positive class (log_num over the equivalent set), divides occurrence mass by
class size, and masks equivalent pairs out of the ring, so both of those gradients disappear. That
is a mechanism aimed at exactly the read step comp_ca scores.

THE NEW MECHANISM (target axis): shrunk ZCA metric on the observation target

target__r2_shrunk_zca_content_metric.py estimates the mean mu and covariance C of the training
targets themselves -- accumulated from z_obs only, never from the model's predictions, so there is
no feedback loop -- over the first 192 training steps (up to 384 target rows per step, taken by a
deterministic stride so no RNG is consumed and the batcher/dropout streams are untouched). It then
forms, once, the symmetric operator

    C = U diag(lam) U^T,  lam_bar = mean(lam)
    g_i = ( lam_bar / (lam_i + rho*lam_bar) ) ^ (gamma/2)        gamma = 1.0, rho = 0.08
    g   = g * clamp( sqrt( sum(lam) / sum(g^2 * lam) ), 0.5, 2.0 )
    W   = U diag(g) U^T,      W^-1 = U diag(1/g) U^T

and from then on trains on make_target(z_obs) = (z_obs - mu) W, frozen for the rest of the run.
to_obs(pred) = pred W^-1 + mu is the exact algebraic inverse, so the retrieval eval still ranks
candidates by plain squared L2 in the untouched observation space; nothing about the metric, the
eligible slice or the candidate sets changes.

WHAT THIS COMPUTES THAT NEITHER PARENT COMPUTES

Neither program changes the geometry of the regression target: both train MSE-like and contrastive
terms in the per-coordinate-standardized observation basis, whose covariance is strongly
anisotropic (standardization equalizes coordinate variances but leaves the correlations, so the top
eigen-directions still hold most of the energy). Training in W-space is exactly training under the
Mahalanobis metric ||W(p - z)||^2 = (p-z)^T C^-gamma_shrunk (p-z): every independent factor of
variation of the observation distribution gets comparable loss weight instead of weight
proportional to its variance. The eigenvalue floor rho bounds the largest gain at
sqrt(1/rho) ~ 3.5 times the scale factor, so no direction is amplified without limit, and the
single global rescale keeps the total target variance unchanged so the objective's absolute
constants (_TEMP 0.25, _MARGIN 0.5, _DUP_DELTA 0.05) stay in the regime they were tuned in. The
ZCA (symmetric) form rather than PCA whitening is deliberate: it is the whitening closest to the
identity, so the arch's direct additive copy path (direct_gate * target_read, tr_read, the FiLM
views) stays approximately aligned with what it has to emit.

WHY THAT SHOULD MOVE comp_ca

comp_ca is decided by which of the N in-window contents the prediction is nearest to. The part of a
target that decides that is the candidate's offset from the window's own centroid -- the N contents
in a cups window are all short config-like files, so what they share (it is a file dump, not a
directory listing, not an empty mv observation) is large and cancels in the comparison, and what
separates them is small. Under an identity target, a model that emits the window centroid already
captures most of the available squared-error reduction, and routing the right content buys little
extra loss; that is the regime in which a name-keyed or order-keyed shortcut is as good as tracking.
Under the whitened target the shared coarse component sits in high-lam directions and is shrunk,
while the identity offset sits in lower-lam directions and is amplified, so the loss advantage of
actually routing the right content over emitting the centroid grows by roughly the ratio of the
gains. I checked this on a synthetic distribution with a decaying spectrum and windows that share
their top-48 latent factors: the within-window identity residual's share of target energy went from
0.109 to 0.722 (numbers from a synthetic construction, NOT a measurement of this eval or of any
model). A second, smaller effect: the objective's per-coordinate precision weight
w = (1/(mse_d+eps))^0.5 upweights coordinates the model already fits; in the decorrelated basis
mse_d is far more uniform, so that "focus on what you can already predict" prior is largely
neutralized.

VERIFIED PROPERTIES OF THE CODE (checked numerically on synthetic data, not on the real eval)

  - round-trip to_obs(make_target(z)) - z has relative error ~8e-7 (float32 representation limit;
    W and W^-1 are built in float64 from one eigendecomposition and cast once).
  - W is exactly symmetric; gains bounded, condition number ~20 on the synthetic spectrum.
  - bit-identical targets stay bit-identical after the transform, so the quotient objective's
    equivalence test (tt_raw <= 1e-5) still finds the silent-step class and the expose/read pair.
  - total target variance preserved to ~1%.
  - identity until step 192 and frozen after; no RNG drawn; no parameters registered (buffers
    only, so the optimizer's key_d / (D,D) routing and the spectral cap see nothing new).
  - apply cost 2.3 ms per step for 4096 rows on CPU; the accumulation phase is 192 steps and the
    single eigendecomposition is ~1 s. Speed impact on the fixed step budget is small; this is a
    FLOP/wall-clock estimate off-harness, not a measurement of the real training loop.

WHAT WOULD HAVE TO BE TRUE FOR IT TO FAIL

  1. The alignment assumption. If what separates the N in-window contents does NOT sit in
     below-average-variance directions of the global target distribution -- e.g. if a JSON blob and
     a shell script differ mostly along the same top eigen-directions that separate them from an
     empty observation -- then whitening shrinks the discriminative component instead of amplifying
     it and comp_ca gets worse, not better. This is the load-bearing assumption and I cannot check
     it from inside the jail.
  2. Representability. The whitened target is a fixed linear image of the copyable quantity, so the
     model must learn W on top of routing. The only full-rank D x D learnable path onto a retrieved
     observation is tr_read, which the optim spectrally caps at 4.0, while the scale-clamped max
     gain can reach ~5. If that clip binds, part of the transform has to be absorbed by the rank-176
     head and the rank-192 composition renderer, and the copy path gets noisier.
  3. Amplified directions being un-learnable rather than merely under-weighted. The frozen encoder
     is deterministic, so there is no observation noise, but a trailing direction may encode e.g. an
     unpredictable canary hex string. At a read step that content is copyable from context, so it
     should be learnable there; at the many non-read steps it is not, and capacity spent there is
     wasted.
  4. The step budget. If total steps were below 192 the transform never activates and the candidate
     degrades exactly to the plain crossover (safe, but then the target axis contributes nothing).
  5. The eval must route the prediction through the module's own to_obs, as the axis contract
     states. Module-level make_target/to_obs are provided as a self-consistent identity pair so a
     path that ignored LEARNED would train and evaluate in the same (identity) space rather than in
     mismatched spaces.
