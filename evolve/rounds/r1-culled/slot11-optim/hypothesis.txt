CANDIDATE: r23 imagination-composition renderer + power-law consolidation optimiser
AXES CHANGED: arch (new), optim (new, my assigned axis). objective / batcher / head inherited
unchanged. The head is inherited from the PARENT and is already a strict superset of the
PARTNER's head (r20_masked_window_imagination_consistency contains the r18 transition-forward-
model aux verbatim and adds the imagination aux), so the head crossover is satisfied by keeping
the parent's. Both parents already carry the same optim, so the optim crossover is "keep it
whole and add".

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE MECHANISM — what this code computes that neither parent does

ARCH (arch__r23_imagination_composition_renderer.py). One class on the shared r18 path-state
latent-transition trunk, inheriting from the parent's R20EndpointImaginationWorldModel, doing
two things at once:

  (a) PARTNER'S MECHANISM, KEPT: the r22 composition renderer verbatim — a zero-init,
      RMS-capped MLP over [r_obs, r_view, read_path, h_cmd0, r_ctx, s_cmd] added into
      target_read through a sigmoid gate, so the three retrieved-content channels can interact
      vector-wise instead of only being scalar-mixed by read_mix.

  (b) PARENT'S MECHANISM, PROMOTED FROM DEAD TO LIVE: in the parent, the imaginer is reachable
      only inside `if bool(missing_pair.any())`, and the parent's own source states that on
      training and fitness streams missing_pair is identically False. So on every scored
      forward the parent's r20 arch is bit-for-bit the r18 arch, and the imaginer is a
      disconnected sub-network trained by the head aux and never read. `_imagine_stream` makes
      it a channel that fires at EVERY command position j:
        m_j  = max{ m < j : cos(cmd_m, cmd_j) > 0.60 and cmd_m valid }        (hard argmax)
        mask = { pair p : p < m_j and pair p observed }
        a_m  = MHA(query = qm(cmd_{m_j}), keys/values = k_proj/v_proj([cmd_p ; obs_p]), mask)
        a_r  = MHA(query = qr(cmd_j),     same keys/values,                     mask)
        out  = mlp(LN([a_m ; a_r ; tanh(qf([cmd_{m_j} ; cmd_j]))]))  , zeroed where mask is empty
        pred_cmd_j += sigmoid(imag_gate([h_cmd0_j ; read_feat_j])) * (out * imag_gain)
      imag_gain is a zero-init (D,) parameter, so the whole channel contributes exactly 0 at
      init and the model starts as r18+comp-renderer. The m_j rule is the inverse of the rule
      the inherited head uses to MINE its imagination training pairs (it pairs each k with the
      nearest LATER same-path j at the same 0.60 cosine threshold), so the eval-time query
      distribution is the training-time one, read backwards.
      Also: k_proj/v_proj are computed once per pair and shared across all query positions, so
      the whole-stream read costs one extra attention, not one per position.
      The imaginer's internal widths are reduced (dk 192->128, dv 256->192, qf 256->192,
      hid 512->384) so total parameter count is 5.239M vs the parent's 5.241M — the crossover
      adds the renderer without growing the model.

OPTIM (optim__r23_powerlaw_consolidation_capped_readout.py). The parent's optimiser is carried
whole — AdamW(warmup/hold/cosine-to-floor) + Muon on the (key_d x d) addressing projections
with the sibling rule + power-iteration spectral cap on (D,D) readouts — and one new stage runs
between the parameter update and the cap. Every parameter carries a Benna-Fusi style cascade of
m=4 exponential iterate averages
        u_k <- (1 - a_k) u_k + a_k p ,   a_k = 0.25 * 4^-k   (windows ~4, 16, 64, 256 steps)
lazily initialised to p at 30% of training. In the last 30% of steps the live weight is pulled
onto the capacity-weighted mixture
        ubar = sum_k w_k u_k ,   w_k proportional to 2^k   (w = 1/15, 2/15, 4/15, 8/15)
        p <- (1 - beta_t) p + beta_t ubar ,  beta_t = 0.04 * smoothstep(tail progress)
so the deployed weight becomes a POWER-LAW trailing average of its own trajectory (a mixture of
exponential windows spanning two orders of magnitude) rather than one minibatch-noisy iterate.
Two structural properties I checked by construction, not by measurement:
  - the pull is a convex combination of averages of p, so its DC gain in the drift direction is
    1: under a constant drift it costs a one-off positional lag, not a rate reduction. A
    straight Benna-Fusi cascade on the WEIGHTS (input into u_1, coupling back from u_2) would
    NOT have this property: conservation of sum_k C_k u_k forces the steady-state drift rate of
    the deployed weight down by the total capacity (15x at m=4). That is why the cascade here
    is over averages with a rare pull, not over the weights themselves.
  - the pull is confined to the last 30% of steps, where the schedule is already between 0.2
    and 0.05 of peak LR, so whatever closed-loop drag remains is paid where there is least
    optimisation progress left to lose.
Deterministic, no RNG drawn, no global RNG touched, scheduler contract unchanged.

2. WHY THIS SHOULD MOVE comp_ca

The score is a paired difference between the native move chain and a chain in which two files
swapped their moves, over the SAME board and the same queried location. Anything keyed on the
queried name, or on move order, is identical in both arms and cancels. Only a quantity that
changes when the two files' CONTENTS are routed differently can score.

The imaginer is, of the mechanisms in either parent, the only module trained explicitly on the
map (contents of the prefix before the mutation, the mutating command, the reading command) ->
the content actually observed at the read. The head supervises it with an eval-geometry
L2-InfoNCE against obs_j on mined (k -> next same-path j) pairs. That is precisely
"carry a file's identity from where it was shown, through the mutation, to the read", learned
as one function instead of as a chain of delta-rule writes. Under the role swap, the prefix
pair contents entering `mask` are unchanged but the mutating command c_m differs, so a_m, the
qf term, and hence `out` differ — the channel's contribution is a function of BOTH the routing
and the contents, so it does not cancel. The parent structurally cannot express any of this at
eval time (the branch is unreachable on both-valid even-length streams); the partner has no
imaginer at all. This is the one thing the crossover can say that neither parent can.

The delta-rule + latent-transition machinery reaches a read at hop depth n by composing n
writes; the imaginer reaches it in ONE cross-attention hop conditioned on the endpoint pair.
Those two routes fail differently, and read_mix / imag_gate let the model weight them per
position. On the reported window mix (d3 n=60, d4+ n=29, d2 n=0) the composed route is exactly
where error accumulates, which is where an independent single-hop route should pay.

The composition renderer contributes the partner's half: the three retrieved-content channels
currently combine only through a scalar softmax over 3 weights, which cannot express "take the
identity from channel A but the location context from channel B". A gated MLP over the
concatenation can, and it is zero-init so it costs nothing if it is not used.

The optimiser change is the smaller lever and I say so. Its argument is that composition error
is multiplicative in depth: if the deployed transition/readout parameters sit at theta* + delta
with delta the residual minibatch noise, the error at a read behind n hops grows with n (and
faster if the recurrence gain exceeds 1), so iterate noise costs deep windows more than shallow
ones — and comp_ca is measured only on deep windows. Trailing-averaging the tail of the
trajectory shrinks delta with essentially no optimisation cost. In a synthetic noisy
least-squares probe (NOT the real harness, and explicitly not extrapolated to it) the
consolidation reduced final parameter MSE by 25-40% across pull in [0.03, 0.08] and tail in
[0.15, 0.40], and never increased it; the defaults sit in the middle of that plateau.

3. WHAT I EXPECT TO SEE IF I AM WRONG, AND THE CHEAPEST OBSERVATION

Cheapest observation, three numbers taken from one trained net, no extra training:
  (i)   ||imag_gain||_inf and the mean of sigmoid(imag_gate(.)) at scored command positions.
        If the gain is below ~1e-3 the imagination channel learned to switch itself off, the
        arch change is a no-op, and comp_ca should be at parent level (+0.011) with the run
        merely ~20% slower. That is the single clearest falsification.
  (ii)  the fraction of scored command positions where `live` is True (an m_j above the 0.60
        cosine threshold exists AND at least one observed pair precedes it). If that fraction
        is near zero on the deep windows, the m-selection rule is mismatched to real shell
        commands ("cat B" not within cosine 0.60 of "mv A B"), the channel emits zero exactly
        where the score is measured, and again nothing moves. I would then expect a lower
        threshold, or selection on the arch's own tr_path key geometry rather than raw command
        cosine, to be the fix — not abandoning the channel.
  (iii) final training loss versus the parent's at the same step count. If consolidation is the
        problem, that loss is visibly higher and the whole candidate underperforms uniformly
        (shallow and deep alike) rather than only on deep windows.
If comp_ca lands between +0.011 and the 0.0527 bar with a healthy gain and a healthy `live`
fraction, the mechanism works but is under-trained: the imagination aux ramps over 400 steps
and only ever sees mined pairs, so the channel would want a larger imag_weight, not a redesign.

4. THE RISK I SEE IN MY OWN DESIGN

The biggest one is train/eval distribution mismatch for the promoted imaginer, compounded by a
shortcut. The imaginer is trained by the head only on pairs the head mines, weighted by
sim_kj * (0.15 + mutation gate); at eval it is queried at every command position through a hard
argmax that I chose to invert the mining rule but which is not the same selection (nearest
earlier same-path from j is only the same as nearest later same-path from k when no third
same-path command sits between them). Where they disagree, the channel is queried off its
training distribution and can inject confident nonsense into pred_cmd. Worse, once imag_gain
leaves zero the MAIN loss back-propagates into the imaginer for the first time, and the
imaginer has a name-only escape hatch: the tanh(qf([c_m ; c_r])) branch needs no prefix content
at all. The main loss could ride that branch, which is exactly a queried-name feature and
cancels to zero in comp_ca, while degrading the aux's clean composition solution. I mitigated
the most obvious form of this by zeroing the entire channel when the prefix mask is empty (so
the name-only path can never be the sole signal), and by the zero-init gain that hands control
over gradually — but I did not prove the shortcut cannot be learned, and I could not measure
any of this without the real harness.

Second risk, smaller: this is a two-axis change, so a null result does not tell the search
whether the renderer, the promoted imaginer, or the consolidation was at fault.
