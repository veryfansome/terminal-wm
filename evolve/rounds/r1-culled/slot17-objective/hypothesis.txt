CANDIDATE: objective r1_trajectory_transport_recall + arch r1_redisplay_occluded_composition_renderer
AXES CHANGED: objective, arch.  Inherited unchanged: optim, target, batcher, stream, head.

THE CROSSOVER
The two parents differ on exactly one axis: the parent's arch is the r22 composition renderer
(extra capacity to COMBINE retrieved channels into target_read), the partner's arch is r22
observation-occlusion denoising (train-time removal of obs tokens, so prediction from missing
evidence is in-distribution). I keep both: my arch subclasses the composition renderer and adds
occlusion inside its forward, and I make the occlusion CONTENT-TARGETED rather than uniform. My
assigned axis, objective, carries the new mechanism and is built from the SAME statistic the new
occlusion uses, so the two changes are one design and not two.


1. MECHANISM — what the code computes that the parents do not

(a) The objective recovers per-trajectory segments from the flattened command rows. The harness
builds ctx["prev"] as torch.cat([zeros, tgt[:, :-1]]) before masking, so the first command row of
every sequence in the batch — and only that row — has an exactly-zero prev. Setting WANTS_CTX and
thresholding ||prev||^2 <= 1e-6 therefore reconstructs the batch's sequence boundaries exactly
(verified: on batches of sequences of length 4,5,6,7 the recovered segment sizes are 4,5,6,7). Rows
are contiguous per sequence, so within-segment position differences are just row-index differences.

(b) From the target-target distance matrix tt (already computed for the inherited ring) it forms,
for each row i, the nearest EARLIER target in the same segment: dmin_i, its hop distance hop_i, and
the distance to the immediately preceding target dprev_i. With a batch-adaptive scale sigma =
0.35 * mean(within-segment earlier-pair tt) it builds a detached row weight

    u_i = 1 + 3 * exp(-dmin_i/sigma) * (1 - exp(-dprev_i/sigma)) * (1 - exp(-(hop_i-1)/1.5)),
    u normalised to mean 1.

Read literally: a row is upweighted when its answer REPEATS content that was displayed earlier in
the same window (recall), is NOT the content displayed one step ago (surprise), and the display it
repeats sits several steps back (depth). On a hand-built window [A,B,C,mv,mv,A,B,B] the measured u
is 0.59-0.66 on the first five rows and on the immediate repeat, and 2.18 on the two rows whose
content came back from five hops away — a 3.7x concentration on exactly the deep-carry rows.

(c) It adds a WITHIN-TRAJECTORY listwise term: for each row, a softmax over -dist2(pred_i, tgt_j)/T
restricted to j in the same segment (near-duplicate targets are suppressed by the inherited
1-exp(-tt/delta) mask), cross-entropy against j = i, weighted by u. dist2 is the eval's own
per-dimension-mean squared L2, in the inherited precision-weighted metric.

(d) It adds a prev-centred differential term: 1 - cos(pred_i - prev_i, tgt_i - prev_i), gated by how
large the true change is, weighted by u.

(e) The inherited ring-negative focal listwise, the MSE anchor and the gated global repulsion hinge
are kept verbatim; the only change to them is that the focal listwise is now weighted by u.

(f) The arch drops each obs token during training with probability
p_t = ramp * (0.10 + 0.45 * exp(-dmin_t/sigma)), where dmin_t is the distance from obs_t to the
nearest EARLIER valid obs in the same row — i.e. the same recall statistic as (b), computed inside
the forward. Measured drop rate on the window above: 0.10-0.13 on novel observations, 0.53-0.56 on
re-displays. The dropped token is zeroed and key-padded, which under the r18 trunk also zeroes the
pair's write gate, so an occluded observation is simply never written to the delta-rule memories.


2. WHY THIS SHOULD MOVE comp_ca

The metric is a paired difference over the SAME board: native chain minus role-swapped chain. Every
term common to both chains cancels. What does not cancel is which stored content the model places at
the queried location, and the swap's confusion set is precisely {the contents of the other files in
that same window}. Two structural consequences:

- MSE and any full-batch contrastive spend most of their gradient on what all candidate observations
  of a window share (cwd line, exit code, output formatting, system identity). A softmax over
  distances to a fixed candidate set is invariant to a common translation of the prediction and all
  candidates, so the within-trajectory term (c) has ZERO gradient on that shared component and all
  of its gradient on the relative arrangement — the same quantity the paired difference isolates.
  The parents cannot express this: their negative set is the whole flattened batch (roughly 1000
  rows, of which only ~15 are same-window), and the ring reweighting is a function of target-target
  distance alone, which cannot tell a same-window confuser from a cross-system row at the same
  distance. Same-window confusers are a ~1.5% minority of the parents' negatives; here they are
  100% of the local term's negatives.

- Deep windows (d3, d4+ = all 89 scored windows) are the ones where the content at the query was
  last displayed several hops back. Rows of that shape exist in ordinary shell trajectories (a file
  is cat'ed, other things happen, it is cat'ed again) but are a small minority, and under a uniform
  row weighting their gradient is swamped by rows solvable by copying the previous observation. (b)
  detects them from the target statistics alone and multiplies their gradient by up to ~4x while
  explicitly giving hop-1 repeats no bonus — the shortcut the metric cancels gets no extra pull.

- (f) manufactures more of them. Occluding a re-display forces the later prediction of that same
  content to reach past the removed copy to the original display, converting a d2 example into a
  d3+ example in the model's input while leaving the target untouched. The parent's arch alone has
  the capacity to compose retrieved channels but is trained on a corpus whose typical carry distance
  is short; the partner's arch alone increases carry distance but at random, mostly deleting novel
  evidence that nothing can recover. Targeting the occlusion at redundant copies is what makes the
  union more than a union, and it uses the same statistic the loss uses to score the resulting rows.

- (d) supervises the direction of the change relative to the current state, which is the quantity the
  r18 delta-rule memories and the latent transition operator actually carry, and which the inherited
  head aux already supervises off-line; the objective now supervises it on the main path too.

CAUSALITY. ctx["prev"] and ctx["cmd"] are the harness's declared causal side-channels. The occlusion
decision for obs_t is a function of obs_{<=t} and changes the token stream only at index 2t+1, which
under the causal mask is invisible to every command position <= t; command position t+1 legitimately
sees obs_t's absence. Verified: stream.leakage_ok passes on a trained net, and eval forward is
occlusion-free (self.training is False) and bit-deterministic.

ANTI-COLLAPSE. Measured on n=60 random targets: a zero prediction scores 7.358, the target mean
scores 7.357, a near-true prediction scores 0.008. Both listwise terms are cross-entropies over
distinct targets, which no constant can minimise; u is a function of the targets only, so it cannot
be gamed by the prediction.


3. WHAT I EXPECT IF I AM WRONG, AND THE CHEAPEST OBSERVATION THAT SHOWS IT

If the mechanism is right, comp_ca rises and the rise is concentrated in the d4+ bucket, while
next-obs retrieval health stays at or near the parent's (the local term is aligned with, not opposed
to, the global one).

If I am WRONG, the most likely shapes are:
  (i) The within-trajectory term is redundant with the global one because same-window observations
      are already the batch's nearest neighbours under the ring. Signature: comp_ca within noise of
      the parent's +0.0300 and retrieval health unchanged. Cheapest check: log, for one training
      batch, the fraction of each row's top-32 ring-weighted negatives that are same-segment. If
      that fraction is already high (>0.3), the term adds little and the design is a wash.
  (ii) The transport weighting is measuring the wrong rows. Cheapest check: dump u alongside the
      (segment, position, hop) triple for one real batch and eyeball whether the high-u rows are
      re-reads of earlier file contents rather than, say, every `cd` back to a directory already
      visited. If most high-u rows are cwd repeats, u is tracking navigation, not file identity, and
      comp_ca will not move.
  (iii) The targeted occlusion removes so much of the recoverable evidence that training degrades.
      Signature: next-obs retrieval health drops materially versus the parent while comp_ca does
      not rise. Cheapest check: the training loss curve — if it plateaus higher than the parent's
      after the ramp completes at step 1000, occ_p_redisplay=0.55 is too aggressive.
No performance number for this candidate is measured; everything above is either an algebraic
property of the code or a measurement on synthetic tensors, and I did not run the real harness.


4. THE RISK I SEE IN MY OWN DESIGN

The single biggest risk is (ii) above in a specific form: in shell trajectories a large share of
"the observation repeats an earlier observation" cases are not file-content transport at all but
`cd` returning to a previously visited directory, or a command whose output is empty/identical
boilerplate. render_obs puts cwd and exit code in every observation, so two unrelated steps in the
same directory with short outputs can be near-identical in embedding space. Those rows would receive
high u and high occlusion probability while carrying no file identity, diluting the mechanism at
best and, at worst, training the model to reproduce cwd strings from memory — a behaviour that is
identical under native and swapped chains and therefore cancels to exactly zero in comp_ca while
consuming most of the reweighted gradient. The dupmask partially protects the loss (an exact
duplicate is removed as a negative) but does NOT protect u, which uses dmin directly, nor the
occlusion, which targets exactly those rows. Mitigations already in the code are weak: sigma is
batch-adaptive rather than absolute, and the surprise factor removes only the hop-1 case. A safer
variant would key u on the residual tgt - prev rather than on tgt, so that the shared cwd/format
component is removed before the nearest-earlier search; I did not do that because the residual of a
re-display is not stable across different intervening observations, and I judged the direct content
match the more faithful surrogate for the swap test. This is the assumption most likely to be wrong.

Second risk: the objective now has four additive terms and their relative weights (_TRAJ_W = 1.0,
_DIFF_W = 0.15, _TRANSPORT_KAPPA = 3.0) are chosen from magnitude reasoning, not tuning — the global
listwise starts near log(n) ~ 7 and the local one near log(15) ~ 2.7, so the local term is roughly a
third of the total pressure at init. If the true optimum needs the local term to dominate, this
candidate under-shoots rather than fails.

Cost: the objective adds ~14 ms/iteration over the parent's ~24 ms at n=1216 on CPU (no new matmul —
the segment work reuses the dist2 and tt matrices the parent already builds); the occlusion adds one
B x n_pair x n_pair batched matmul per step. Both are small against the arch's per-step recurrence.
