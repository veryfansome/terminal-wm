CANDIDATE: crossover of g0-16-retrieval-composition (parent) with the r21 evidence-gated
imagination-write / algebraic-observer design (partner).
AXES CHANGED: optim (assigned), arch. head is taken from the partner unchanged; objective and
batcher are identical in both parents and are inherited unchanged.

1. MECHANISM — what this code computes that neither parent does

ARCH (r23_transition_fed_composition_imagwrite). The class is the C3 join of the parent's
R22RetrievalCompositionRenderer and the partner's R21EvidenceGatedImagWrite, so it runs r22's
forward with r21's evidence-gated `_transition_reads`. On top of the join it adds ONE wire: the
per-path latent-transition read `tr_reads` becomes a fourth D-wide input to the composition
renderer (comp_in_dim goes 3D -> 4D, comp_ln/comp_in are rebuilt at that width; comp_out stays
zero-init and RMS-capped, comp_gate is untouched). In both parents `tr_reads` reaches the
prediction only through the linear zero-init (D,D) `tr_read` map added at the very end; here it
can also be composed nonlinearly with r_obs, r_view and read_path before target_read is formed.

OPTIM (r23_zeroinit_channel_boost_stiefel_addressing_polyak). Keeps the parents' shared skeleton
(AdamW + Muon on the sibling-paired (key_d x d) addressing projections + a spectral cap on (D,D)
readouts + warmup-hold-cosine) and adds three things:
 (a) ZERO-INIT CHANNEL DETECTION. At make() time — which the harness calls after head.wrap and
     after net.to(device) — every parameter that is exactly zero is split out of the main AdamW
     group. Zero MATRICES get weight_decay 0 and lr * zero_lr_mult (2.0); zero VECTORS get
     weight_decay 0 at the base lr. Nothing about the arch is hard-coded; exact-zero is the
     detector.
 (b) STIEFEL PULL. After every optimizer step (post-warmup) each Muon-routed addressing matrix W
     gets one relaxed Bjorck step, W <- W + (eta/2)(W - (WW^T / s) W) with s = tr(WW^T)/rows,
     followed by a rescale back to its original Frobenius norm. Singular values above the RMS
     shrink, those below grow, the total energy is held fixed; the fixed point is a flat singular
     spectrum. It is sync-free (no .item() in the step path), as are the rewritten spectral cap
     and Muon guards.
 (c) TAIL POLYAK. The returned scheduler object also maintains an EMA of all parameters over the
     last 40% of `steps` (beta set from the window, horizon window/3) and copies it into the live
     weights once, at the final step. Paired with it, the cosine floor is raised 0.05 -> 0.15, so
     the tail keeps exploring and the averaging does the final annealing instead of the LR decay.

2. WHY THIS SHOULD MOVE comp_ca (carrying identity across hops)

The metric cancels anything keyed on the queried name or on move order, so the only earning path
is: the right file's content must be sitting in the transition memory at the query command, and it
must reach the prediction. This design attacks both, plus the fixed step budget.

ADDRESSING (mechanism b). The delta-rule read for path k is sum_i decay^(k-i) (p_k . p_i) corr_i.
Cross-path leakage is governed entirely by the Gram matrix of the key set, and every key is
L2-normalized downstream — `_unit` for path_read/path_write/tr_path, `_quotient` (which ends in
`_unit`) for file_read/file_write — so the SCALE of these matrices is functionally inert and only
the direction spread matters. A poorly conditioned d->key_d map squashes distinct command features
into nearly parallel keys; the resulting leakage enters every hop and compounds, which is exactly
why it should show up on d3/d4+ windows and not on shallow ones. Semi-orthogonality of a 64x176
matrix constrains only the 64-dim retained subspace and leaves a 112-dim null space, so the pull
does NOT prevent the map from quotienting out the verb direction (this is the algebraic reason the
constraint is cheap here and would not be on a square map). Muon — present in both parents —
orthogonalizes the UPDATE; nothing in either parent conditions the MAP itself. On an untrained net
this pull took the six routed matrices from condition number ~3.7 to ~1.17 in ~40 applications with
Frobenius norm unchanged to three decimals (smoke test, not the real harness).

READOUT / BUDGET (mechanism a). Every channel by which multi-hop content reaches the prediction in
this family is born at exactly zero: tr_read.weight/bias, comp_out.weight/bias, film_out,
sysfilm_out, and the partner head's observer .out.weight. Under the parents' single AdamW group
these grow at trunk LR against weight_decay 5e-4, which is a force pointing back at zero for
precisely the channels that must switch on. Exact-zero-at-construction is an arch-free detector for
that set, and freeing them from decay plus doubling their LR should bring them on earlier inside a
fixed step budget. If the composition channel is still small when the budget ends, the score cannot
be earned at all.

COMPOSITION (arch wire). The parent renders a nonlinear composition of the retrieval channels but
its renderer cannot see the transition read; the partner fills the transition memory during
unobserved hops (imagined write, gated by accumulated observed-write evidence) but can deliver that
content only through a linear map. Neither parent can express "nonlinear composition of the
imagined multi-hop content with the retrieved prefix content". That is what the new comp_z channel
is. It stays causal: `_transition_reads` appends s_pre (the memory content BEFORE step i's write)
to `reads` and only then folds in obs_i, so tr_reads[:, i] depends on observations strictly before
i; moving its computation earlier in forward() changes nothing it depends on.

GENERALIZATION (mechanism c). The measurement is on windows held out BY IMAGE, and comp_ca is a
paired difference of two nearly-equal quantities, so run-to-run jitter in the learned key geometry
costs directly. Tail averaging with a raised floor is the standard trade of LR annealing for
iterate averaging; it reduces that jitter without shortening the exploration phase.

3. WHAT I EXPECT IF I AM WRONG, AND THE CHEAPEST OBSERVATION

- Addressing claim false: comp_ca will not improve preferentially at depth. Cheapest check is the
  per-depth split already in the eval feedback — if d4+ (n=29) does not gain relative to d3 (n=60),
  the "leakage compounds with depth" story is dead. Second cheapest: the condition numbers of the
  six routed (64,176) matrices at end of a real run; if they are still > 3 the gradient is simply
  overwhelming eta=0.08 and the mechanism never engaged, which is a tuning failure, not a
  refutation.
- Zero-init boost false: expect next-obs retrieval health and the native pick rate to drop below
  the parent's while comp_ca is flat — over-eager injection channels overfitting the fit split.
  The native-pick-vs-chance line reports this directly.
- Polyak/floor false: the whole run shifts rather than the deep windows, and the last training loss
  (post-merge) is worse than the step-before-merge loss. That single comparison is the cheapest
  observation and it also detects the merge silently not firing.
- Renderer wire false: comp_out grows but under role-swap the pick follows the swapped content
  LESS than the parent's — i.e. the renderer learned a shortcut on the prefix rather than on the
  transition content.

4. RISK

Biggest: four changes land at once, and three of them (LR floor, EMA merge, zero-group LR) are
global, so a loss is unattributable. The sharpest single technical risk is a known false positive
in the routing rule: `sys_val.weight` is nn.Linear(d=176, sys_d=64) and therefore has the same
(64,176) shape as the five real addressing projections, so the sibling rule routes it to Muon
(already true in the parent) and now also to the Stiefel pull — and unlike the addressing maps its
output is not unit-normalized, it feeds tanh, so for that one tensor the pull is a genuine function
change rather than pure conditioning. The Frobenius-preserving construction bounds the damage (the
RMS gain over random inputs is held fixed, only the spread moves), but the constraint is not inert
there and I cannot separate it from a flat parameter list. Secondary: the raised LR floor is only
safe BECAUSE the terminal merge fires; the merge is keyed on the scheduler being stepped `steps`
times, which is what evolve/harness.py::_train does today, but if a caller ever steps fewer times
the run simply ends at a 3x higher LR than the parent's with no averaging — a pure loss. Third: the
r21 head's observer adds ~5.5M parameters on top of a ~1.3M-parameter trunk, so the zero-matrix LR
boost applies to observer.out as well; if the observer is the thing that overfits, the boost makes
it worse.
