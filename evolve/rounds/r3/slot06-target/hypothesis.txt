WHAT THE CROSSOVER IS, AND WHY THESE TWO HALVES

The parent's distinguishing mechanism is its arch: r22_prefix_content_xattention is r18_pathstate_latent_transition_worldmodel (the partner's arch, verbatim, by subclassing) plus a cross-attention channel whose VALUES are the raw 768-d prefix observation embeddings. That is the only machinery in either program that can literally place an earlier observation vector at the current command position, which is what a cups read step has to output. Taking the parent's arch therefore keeps the partner's arch inside it; there is nothing of the partner's arch to give up.

The partner's distinguishing mechanism is its objective: exact-target equivalence classes. Reading evolve/jail_data/train_sample.jsonl, that mechanism is not cosmetic on this data, it repairs a specific broken gradient. render_obs is "cwd=... exit=...\n" + output, and in a cups window the expose step (cat /tmp/w/cups/g53/f149.tmp) and the later test step (cat /tmp/w/cups/usr/share/misc/magic.txt.1) produce byte-identical observation text, so their z_obs rows are the identical vector, and both rows are in the same training batch because they are steps of the same sequence. Under r12 the expose row is an in-batch NEGATIVE for the test row with a target vector equal to the test row's own positive: dist2 to it equals dist2 to the positive, so the softmax over candidates can never put more than ~1/2 on the true one, and the pull toward the true target is scaled by (1 - p_true - p_dup) ~ 0. The rows the metric is actually about are the rows r12 starves of gradient. The same thing happens en masse for the empty-output steps ("cwd=/ exit=0\n" from every mv and every redirect op, a large fraction of all steps), which are all mutually identical across the whole corpus. r22's quotient makes the class a multi-positive (log_num aggregates over it) and divides occurrence mass by class size, so the read step gets a real gradient and the hundreds of duplicate empty observations stop counting as hundreds of independent examples.

WHAT THE NEW TARGET MECHANISM COMPUTES

Neither program touches the target: both train the model to predict z_obs under whatever isotropic-in-coordinates metric the loss imposes. My impl replaces the target with an exactly-invertible linear reparametrization M = I + V^T (G - I) V, where V is a rank-32 orthonormal basis and G is a diagonal of positive gains. make_target returns M z_obs; to_obs returns (I + V^T (G^-1 - I) V) pred, which is M^-1 exactly whenever V V^T = I and G > 0, for ANY V and G, converged or not. Measured on a synthetic run: round-trip relative error 7e-7, basis orthonormality error 8e-7.

The content of the mechanism is how G is chosen. During a 600-update warmup the module accumulates, from the training batches only, two EMA second moments of the target rows:
  - the TOTAL moment, and
  - the WITHIN-TRAJECTORY moment, i.e. of each row minus the mean of its own trajectory.
It recovers trajectory boundaries without any side channel: the harness builds prev as a strict causal shift with zeros at each sequence's first step, so a row whose z_prev is exactly the zero vector starts a new sequence, and cmd_mask selects rows contiguously in (batch element, step) order. Rows are weighted by 1/(exact-duplicate count), estimated with a quantized 8-way random projection, so the flood of identical empty observations contributes one unit of mass in total instead of hundreds - the same quotient view the crossover's objective takes, applied to the statistics.
V is carried by orthogonal iteration on the total moment (re-orthonormalized every 4 updates by twice-repeated Gram-Schmidt); the gain on direction v_i is g_i = exp(clamp(0.5 * alpha * (log r_i - mean_j log r_j), +-log 3)) with r_i = (v_i^T W v_i) / (v_i^T T v_i), the fraction of that direction's variance that occurs WITHIN a trajectory rather than between trajectories. Gains start at 1 (the map is exactly the identity at step 0), ramp in over 400 updates, and freeze at 600 so the map the model was last trained under is the map the eval inverts.

Net effect: directions along which observations differ inside one trajectory are amplified in target space, and directions that only separate one system/trajectory from another are compressed, by at most 3x either way, with the log-mean of the gains held at zero so the overall loss scale and the loss's per-coordinate precision weighting are not moved. On a synthetic corpus with planted structure the estimator does exactly this: purely-between directions converged to gain 0.35, purely-within directions to 1.42.

WHY THIS SHOULD MOVE comp_ca

comp_ca is decided by an N-way forced-foil choice in which the foils are the OTHER contents exposed in the SAME window, i.e. in the same trajectory. Whether the model wins a pair is whether its error e satisfies 2 e.(c_j - c_true) < ||c_j - c_true||^2 - the only part of the error that matters is its projection onto WITHIN-TRAJECTORY difference directions. Training under the identity target spends the model's error budget where the observation distribution has variance, and most of that variance is between trajectories: which image, which kind of command, empty vs listing vs file body vs binary junk. A model can drive the training loss down a long way by predicting "an observation of this shape on this system" and remain at chance among the five file contents of one cups window, which is precisely the state the current scores (near zero, on both sides of zero) describe. The equalizer changes the metric the loss is computed in - and only the metric, because the eval un-applies it exactly - so the same gradient budget is spent on the directions the forced-foil comparison actually reads. Together with the objective half, which is what lets the read step receive a gradient at all, the two changes hit the same failure from the two sides: the read rows now get a signal, and the signal is expressed in the coordinates the measurement uses.

WHAT WOULD HAVE TO BE TRUE FOR THIS TO FAIL

1. If the contents of one window differ mainly along the global top principal directions of the observation distribution - i.e. if between-trajectory and within-trajectory variation share their directions - then r_i is nearly constant across the basis, all gains sit near 1, and the target is nearly the identity. In that case the candidate reduces to the plain crossover and any change is the objective's alone. This is the benign failure.
2. The harmful failure: the compressed directions are amplified by 1/g <= 3 when to_obs inverts the map, so if the model responds to the cheaper loss along those directions by letting its error there grow more than 3x, eval-space error along them grows and adds noise to comparisons whose candidate differences have a component there. alpha=1.0 with a 3x cap and rank 32 of 768 bounds this; a larger cap or a full-rank whitening would not.
3. If a trajectory's within-variance is dominated by irrelevant step-to-step variety (an ls listing vs a cat body vs an empty mv result) rather than by content-vs-content differences, the amplified subspace is only loosely aligned with what the foils test and the effect shrinks toward case 1. The duplicate down-weighting removes the largest such contaminant (the empty-observation direction) but not all of them.
4. If the scoring instrument does not route a LEARNED target through net.target_module.to_obs, the prediction is scored un-inverted and the candidate is mis-measured. The contract states the learned pair is evaluated through its own inverse; the module deliberately exposes no module-level make_target/to_obs, so a probe that ignored the learned path would raise rather than silently score the wrong thing.

WHAT THIS DELIBERATELY DOES NOT DO

The target module never sees a command, a path, a verb, a step index, or any move; it only sees the observation embeddings it is asked to transform and the previous-observation tensor, from which it reads a single bit per row (is this the start of a sequence). It cannot know which content was routed where, and it hands the model no information about it: it applies one global, frozen, invertible linear map to every row identically. Because that map is a bijection, it also preserves the objective's exact-equality classes exactly - identical rows stay identical (verified: transformed duplicate distance is exactly 0.0) - which is why this target and this objective can be used together at all, and why a delta-style target could not.
