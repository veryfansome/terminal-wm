AXES CHANGED: objective only (board_transport_permutation_matching, replacing
r22_exact_target_equivalence_quotient). arch, optim, target, batcher, stream and head are the
parent's verbatim. The mechanism needs no other axis: it consumes only pred, tgt and the ctx the
harness already offers under WANTS_CTX, and the parent's stream (baseline_interleave) already
exposes extract_cmd_input, so ctx["cmd"]/ctx["prev"] arrive without touching the stream. It
declares no code_cmds because it does not alter command tokens at all.

MECHANISM
Entropic optimal transport with equipartition (Sinkhorn-Knopp, Cuturi 2013; the balanced-
assignment constraint of Asano et al.'s self-labelling and SwAV; the log-domain unrolled
differentiable assignment of SuperGlue), applied inside each board rather than across the batch.

1. Segmentation. ctx["prev"] is the zero vector exactly at the first command row of every
   sequence and the preceding observation elsewhere, and the harness flattens cmd rows in
   row-major order, so the flattened rows split back into contiguous per-trajectory blocks with
   one comparison. Each block is padded to [n_seg, kmax, D].
2. Cost. Per block, C[i,j] = ||pred_i - tgt_j||^2 / D over that trajectory's own rows. Nothing is
   precision-weighted: this is the same unweighted squared-L2 the probe decides with.
3. Plan. exp(-C/temp) is made doubly stochastic by 15 log-domain Sinkhorn iterations with uniform
   row and column marginals of one, unrolled WITH gradient, masked to the block's valid entries.
   temp is a fixed fraction (0.1) of the mean distinct target-target separation, detached, so
   sharpness tracks the embedding scale instead of a hand-set constant.
4. Duplicates. Numerically equal targets form one class. The plan P[i,j] = 1/m for the m columns
   of row i's class satisfies BOTH marginals exactly, so multi-positives need no special-casing
   and the bijection is not broken by the ~14 empty-output mv rows a 31-step trajectory carries
   (measured on evolve/jail_data/train_sample.jsonl: 29-32 steps, 15-19 distinct outputs, the
   empty string 9-14 times).
5. Loss. Row term = -log of the transported mass a read places on its own content class; column
   term = -log of the mass a content receives from the reads that actually returned it. Both are
   focal-weighted (gamma=1, detached) and weighted by 1/class-size, so the empty-output flood
   weighs as one content instead of fourteen. A 0.1 MSE anchor keeps predictions in the target
   space the probe measures in.

WHY IT SHOULD MOVE comp_ca
A board is a bijection: at every moment each content sits at exactly one location, and the chain
of mv commands permutes that bijection. A row-normalised softmax (the parent, and every
InfoNCE-shaped objective in this lineage) imposes no such constraint — it asks each read
independently "which content is nearest", and a model that answers "the content this location
usually holds", or "the generically most plausible content", is rewarded on every row that
happens to agree. The doubly stochastic constraint makes those answers arithmetically worthless:
because column mass is pinned to one, a prediction that claims the same content for k reads gets
1/k of that column each. In a pure-Python replica of the block math (11-row and 31-row boards,
scratch code, not the real harness) the loss for "always predict the board's most common content"
came out numerically IDENTICAL to the loss for a constant prediction and to the loss for the mean
target — 2.17 and 3.29 on the two boards — while the correct assignment scored 0.007 and a plan
with just two contents exchanged scored 1.05. That identity is the point: the equipartition
constraint removes the generic-content shortcut by construction rather than penalising it with a
heuristic ring kernel, and what is left to gain is exactly the thing comp_ca pays for, namely
knowing WHICH content the chain routed to the location being read.

The second reason to expect movement is the negative set. The parent contrasts a read against
~2000 in-batch rows, almost all from other images and trivially far. This objective contrasts a
read only against the other contents on its own board — which is precisely the N-way forced-foil
choice the probe makes, and precisely the choice that a role swap alters.

PREDICTIONS (all unmeasured; I cannot run the harness from the jail)
- comp_ca above the parent's +0.0262. Directionally I expect the gain to concentrate in the d4+
  windows, where the number of chained moves makes the location prior least informative.
- The native pick rate may move LESS than comp_ca, or even drop slightly, because the objective
  gives up the easy cross-image discrimination the global softmax supplies. The differential is
  what should improve.
- Cost is negligible: the blocks are [64, ~32, ~32], about 65k kernel entries, 15 unrolled
  iterations, versus the parent's dense 2000x2000 pairwise matrix. This should be FASTER per
  step, not slower.

BIGGEST RISK
Absolute calibration. Removing the global contrastive leaves only the 0.1 MSE anchor to place
predictions in the target space; the transport term is invariant to anything that preserves the
within-board cost ordering, so the model could become a good relative discriminator on each board
and a poorly scaled absolute predictor, which would cost the pick-rate reading even if the
differential holds. Secondary risk: segmentation rests on ctx["prev"] being exactly zero only at
sequence starts — true by construction of the harness shift, but a standardized observation
embedding that happened to be the exact zero vector would split a board in two, which degrades
the term to a smaller board rather than corrupting it. Third: temp = 0.1 * mean separation is a
guess; too sharp and the transport gradient concentrates on near-ties, too soft and the perfect
assignment stops scoring near zero (at 0.25 the correct plan already cost 0.40 in the replica).
