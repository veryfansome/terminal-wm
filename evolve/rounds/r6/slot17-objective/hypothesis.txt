MECHANISM: contents are a conserved resource inside one trajectory.

Axes changed: objective only. The parent's other six axes are kept verbatim. The mechanism is a
loss-level term over command rows and needs nothing from the arch, the head, the stream (beyond
extract_cmd_input, which r4_role_bound_path_atom_codes already provides), the batcher or the
optimiser, so changing any of them would be an unjustified confound.

WHAT THE CROSSOVER TAKES FROM EACH SIDE

From the parent (r5_decision_direction_metric_quotient) I keep the whole spine unchanged: the
exact-target-equivalence quotient, the confusability ring, the per-coordinate metric read off the
unit target-difference directions of confusable pairs, the scale-free repulsion hinge in the raw
squared-L2 the eval ranks in, and the class-measured squared-error anchor.

From the crossover partner I take the idea that carries its head impl (rename registry occupancy
routing): a move RE-ADDRESSES a slot rather than copying its value, so a registry holds each
content exactly once and a read returns whatever now occupies the address. The partner spends
parameters and a recurrent scan to build that registry inside the model. I import the same
constraint as a property the LOSS demands of the predictions, with no parameters at all, and I
compute it in the parent's decision-direction metric — that is the join.

THE TWO NEW TERMS (both trajectory-local; segments recovered exactly from ctx["prev"], which is the
zero vector at the first command of every sequence and is strictly causal)

(1) OCCUPANCY TRANSPORT. Within one trajectory the command rows are partitioned into content
classes by exact target equivalence, each class represented by its earliest row. Row-to-content
assignment is scored by an entropic optimal-transport plan (a few log-domain Sinkhorn-Knopp
iterations, differentiated through) under BOTH marginals: uniform over the trajectory's rows, and
per content exactly the number of rows that truly carry it. The loss is the negative log conditional
plan mass on each row's own content. This is the Sinkhorn equipartition trick of self-labelling and
SwAV (Asano et al.; Caron et al.), with equipartition replaced by the trajectory's true occupancy
counts. The point is what a per-row softmax cannot say: a row softmax normalises only over
candidates, so nothing stops every read in a trajectory from claiming the same content, and dumping
a read onto the large empty-output class that all the mv steps share is nearly free. The column
marginal makes each content a resource with a fixed supply — claiming a content another row must
also hold pushes that row off it and both pay, and the supply a content genuinely has must land
somewhere. Rows whose target duplicates an earlier row of the same trajectory under a different
command, with a small class size (the post-chain re-read, and not the empty-output cluster), carry
4x weight.

(2) NAME DECOY. For exactly those re-read rows, the decoy is the earlier row of the same trajectory,
of a different content class, whose command embedding is most similar — the content that this
location's NAME used to hold, rather than the content the chain of moves routed into it. That is the
single confusion the score is built around. A scale-free hinge in the raw unweighted squared-L2
requires (||pred - t_decoy||^2 - ||pred - t_true||^2) / ||t_true - t_decoy||^2, formed algebraically
as 1 + 2 e.(t_true - t_decoy) / ||t_true - t_decoy||^2, to stay above a fixed fraction of one; it is
gated off unless that command similarity stands out above the row's own distribution of similarities
to earlier distinct-content rows, and the separation is floored so a near-duplicate pair cannot
amplify without bound. Where the gate misfires, the term degrades gracefully into an ordinary hard
within-trajectory negative.

WHY THIS SHOULD MOVE comp_ca

comp_ca is a paired difference between a native chain and a role-swapped chain over the same board,
same destinations, same depth. Every predictor that answers from the read location alone gives the
same answer in both arms and contributes exactly zero. The only thing that survives is having
carried a particular content's identity through the moves. The parent's loss is entirely batch-level
geometry: it makes confusable observations separable, but it never asks the model which of the
trajectory's own contents belongs to which of the trajectory's own reads. Both new terms ask exactly
that, and they ask it about the rows the metric is measured on — the read after a silent chain. The
occupancy constraint supplies the pressure a contrastive structurally cannot (a content cannot be
handed to two reads), and the decoy hinge supplies the direction (the routed content beats the
name's previous occupant).

WHAT I EXPECT TO SEE (all unmeasured — no training run exists from the jail)

Higher comp_ca than the parent's +0.0150, driven by native_hit rather than by swap_stayed falling,
and a larger effect on the d4+ windows than on d3, because deeper chains are where the name's
previous occupant is the most attractive wrong answer. The absolute pick rate should not fall: both
terms vanish at a correct prediction (measured on synthetic boards: occupancy 0.04 and decoy 0.08
when perfect, 0.45 and 7.5 when the read claims the name-decoy content), and the class-measured MSE
anchor is untouched. Falsified if comp_ca is at or below the parent's, or if the gain arrives with
swap_stayed rising in step with native_hit, which would mean the model got better at the board and
not at the routing.

MEASURED IN THE JAIL (mechanics only, on synthetic boards shaped like the real cups blocks —
never a performance claim): loss finite and every prediction row receiving gradient for even and odd
trajectory lengths, 1-step and 32-step sequences, all-duplicate targets, predictions at 0, 1e-3, 1,
1e2 and 1e4 scale, float32 and float64; a constant prediction scores 8.67 against 0.29 for a
near-true one, so the loss remains anti-collapse safe; the no-ctx call path returns the parent's
value exactly. With the Sinkhorn iterations forced to zero the transport collapses back to a per-row
softmax and the discrimination the mechanism is for disappears (empty-class claim 0.105 versus
0.294, and the over-claimed row's own penalty falls from 0.12 to 0.00), which is direct evidence the
column marginal, not the added weighting, is doing the work. Cost is +20% wall clock on CPU at the
real row count and should be far less on GPU, since the added work is a batch of RxR problems with
R the trajectory length while the parent's terms are n x n over the whole batch.

RISKS

The occupancy term is satisfiable by a positional shortcut — learning that the k-th read of a
trajectory returns the k-th exposed content, without ever carrying an identity. That is the chain-
position keying the brief warns cancels only in expectation, so it could show up as a positive score
with high shortcut_leaning; the decoy hinge pushes against it but does not forbid it. Secondary
risks: the mined decoy depends on command-embedding similarity, and the r4 stream's path-atom code
occupies the last 128 coordinates with near-orthogonal hashes for sibling paths, so the decoy may
often be some other hard negative instead of the true name predecessor; and two extra terms on top
of an already heavily shaped loss may simply crowd out the parent's listwise term at the weights
chosen (0.5 occupancy, 0.25 decoy).
