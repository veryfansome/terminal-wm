Checking evolve/jail_data/train_sample.jsonl rather than assuming: the destination paths of the
mv chains are drawn from a small shared vocabulary that recurs across trajectories and across

trajectory). In the 24-sequence sample, `cat /tmp/w/cvar/g0/s0.dat` appears in 21 trajectories
and returns 21 different answers; 33 distinct read paths are shared by two or more trajectories.

So the data already contains, for free, the exact dissociation the metric is built on: a
byte-identical read command whose answer is decided only by what the preceding chain put there.
Two sequences that share such a path are a training-time analogue of the native/role-swapped
pair -- same question, different content delivered.

THE MECHANISM

Call the exact destination path string of a routed read (a read whose path is reached by walking
backwards at least one hop through the mv edges written earlier in the same trajectory) a SLOT
KEY. The sequences sharing a slot key form a CONFLICT CLIQUE. The batcher places several members
of one clique in the same batch. Because the objective's listwise contrastive term draws its
negatives from all command positions in the batch, a batch containing four members of a clique
contains four rows whose command embedding is identical and whose target is not. A predictor that
maps command to observation cannot separate them at all; the only information that can is the
move history in the prefix. That is the pressure I want, and it is the same pressure the score
measures, because a predictor keyed on the location being read contributes exactly zero to
comp_ca per window.

Two blocks are not equally worth building. A slot key carried by a fraction p of the split
already collides bs*p times per batch under plain uniform sampling, so blocking a common key buys
nothing. Each clique's quantum is therefore proportional to its COLLISION DEFICIT,
max(eps, block_size - bs*size_c/n), times its number of distinct system images -- cross-image
conflicts are the ones whose resolution has to transfer to the held-out images. Common keys get
near-zero quantum automatically; the rare suffixed cups destinations, which are exactly the read
locations the pack scores, get nearly all of it.

The scheduler is the part borrowed from outside the field. Deficit round robin (Shreedhar and
Varghese, SIGCOMM 1996) is a packet scheduler that gives each queue service proportional to its
quantum with a bounded worst-case deviation, rather than proportional in expectation. Each clique
c holds a deficit D_c; on a step that serves S slots, D_c += q_c * S with q_c the normalized
quantum, and a served clique pays D_c -= m for the m members it contributed. Service goes to the
k = budget/block_size largest deficits. Injection and payment are exactly conserved, so the
deficits stay bounded and every clique's realized share tracks q_c with bounded discrepancy.
Members inside a clique come off a rotating cursor over a seeded permutation, so all members of a
clique get exposed in turn.

The remaining slots are apportioned over four chain-depth strata by Neyman allocation from survey
sampling (Neyman 1934): n_h proportional to N_h * sigma_h^beta, with sigma_h the per-dimension
within-stratum spread of the deepest routed read's observation embedding, so strata whose routed
answers are more heterogeneous get more of the budget. The apportionment is realized by
largest-remainder with a carried remainder vector -- deficit round robin again, on counts -- and
each stratum is drawn from a shuffled epoch without replacement. There is no multinomial and no
iid draw anywhere in the composition; the only randomness is the seeded permutations and a 1e-4
tie-break jitter, all from a private torch.Generator.

WHY THIS SHOULD MOVE comp_ca

comp_ca is the paired difference between the native chain and the role-swapped chain. Anything
the model does that depends only on which location is read cancels to zero by construction. Only
carrying a particular content's identity through the moves survives both arms. Conflict blocks
make the command-to-observation lookup an actively losing strategy on a growing fraction of the
batch, while the base half keeps the rest of the distribution covered so the net does not simply
narrow onto move-heavy trajectories.

The second, quieter claim is about variance rather than pressure. Routed reads are rare: in the
sample, 37 of 365 reads have depth >= 1 and only 12 have depth >= 3. Under the parent's iid
randint plus multinomial, how much deep-chain and how much rare-slot gradient a run receives is a
lottery, and the objective is read on one frozen slice with n=89 windows. Deficit round robin and
epoch sampling remove that lottery: exposure is a bounded-discrepancy realization of the intended
distribution instead of a draw from it. If the parent's +0.0037 sits inside seed noise driven by
that lottery, this should tighten the across-seed spread even where it does not raise the mean.

WHAT I EXPECT TO SEE

comp_ca above the parent's +0.0037, driven by the native arm rising while the swapped-arm
"held" rate does not rise with it -- if both arms move together, the batcher has bought a
first-mover or chain-position shortcut rather than content carrying, and private.shortcut_leaning
should show it. I also expect a smaller across-seed spread than the parent, and no change in
wall-clock: setup parses every sequence once and the per-step work is one topk plus a handful of
slices.

MEASURED IN THE JAIL (mechanical checks only, on synthetic embeddings with the real command
strings; no fitness claim is implied and none of this was run against the real harness):
returns exactly bs indices in range for bs in {1,3,64,256}, including bs > n and bs < block_size;
total_steps = 1; sequences with no mv at all; missing "cmds"; missing "z_obs"; n in {1,2}; and the
extremes of every declared parameter. Bit-identical across repeated construction with the same
seed, different across seeds, leaves the global torch RNG untouched, and does not mutate fit.
3000 steps of composition over 1440 sequences take 0.19 s and setup 0.024 s. On a 1440-sequence
mock built by renaming the per-system files of the real trajectories while keeping the shared slot
vocabulary, the mean number of same-slot-key co-occurring pairs per batch is 3.5x the uniform
sampler's on the rare keys, and every sequence is visited (min 102, max 210 visits over 2000
steps, against a Poisson spread under iid).

UNMEASURED. The fitness effect is unmeasured; the jail cannot run the real harness and this
project has seen miniature probes invert rank in both directions.

BIGGEST RISK

Dilution. The batcher chooses whole sequences, and the loss is flattened over every command
position in the batch -- roughly thirty per sequence -- so the conflict rows are a minority of the
rows even when every block lands. If the real train split is large enough that the scored slot
keys already collide several times per batch under uniform sampling, the collision-deficit
weighting will correctly assign them near-zero quantum and the conflict half will spend itself on
keys the probe never reads, leaving only the exposure-variance effect. The second risk is the
opposite one: at conf_frac_max = 0.6 the majority of each late batch comes from sequences that
contain routed reads, which narrows the training distribution and could cost transfer to the
held-out images; the stratified base half and the cross-image factor in the quantum are the hedge,
and if the candidate lands below its parent, lowering conf_frac_max is the first thing to try.
