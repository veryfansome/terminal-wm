Everything else — arch r4_fingerprint_conditioned_composition, optim r18_spectral_capped_transition_readout,
batcher r4_distinct_blocked_transport_sampler with the parent's params, target identity, stream
baseline_interleave, head r18_transition_forwardmodel_consistency — is kept verbatim. No other axis
needs to move: the mechanism is a second loss term computed from pred/tgt and the causal ctx the
objective contract already offers, and the batcher already blocks batches by image so a board's rows
land together.

MECHANISM. The parent's whole training signal is one global listwise softmax over the entire batch,
whose negatives are picked by target-target proximity (the confusability ring). The measurement is
not that. It is a forced choice among the contents that are on ONE board: N contents were exposed at
N locations of one trajectory, a silent chain of moves shuffles them, one location is read, and the
model is scored on whether it picks the content the chain actually routed there rather than one of
the other contents of that same board. The parent's negatives are drawn from ~1900 rows spanning
~64 trajectories and reweighted by embedding proximity; the roughly thirty rows that constitute the
actual board are a diluted minority of that denominator, and random canary contents are not close in
embedding space, so the ring does not preferentially pick them either. This candidate adds the
missing term: a board-local N-way forced choice on exactly the rows whose answer is decided by a
chain of moves.

Those rows are identified without any label. Trajectory boundaries are recovered exactly from
ctx["prev"], which is the zero vector only at a sequence's first command (the harness builds prev by
shifting tgt one step inside each sequence, and standardized observation embeddings are never
identically zero). A row is a ROUTED READ when its target coincides with the target of an earlier row
of the SAME trajectory that was produced by a DIFFERENT command embedding — the content was already
observed under some other command and has since arrived here — and when that target's batch-wide
coincidence count is at most 16, which excludes the large empty-output cluster (every mv and every
redirect writes it) and stock files that many images share. Checking the data in the jail: in a
trajectory the cups_test read `cat .../sshd_config.cfg.8` returns exactly the content of an earlier
`cat .../g72/f654.dat` expose, and the cvar_test read `cat .../out.dat` returns exactly the content of
an earlier `cat .../s1.dat`; both are picked up by this rule. The no-op re-read (`cat .../s1.dat`
twice with nothing moved in between) has an identical command embedding and is deliberately excluded,
because learning "same command, same answer" from it is the location-keying that cancels to exactly
zero in the metric. Roughly one row in ten qualifies, ~190 rows in a 64-sequence batch.

For those rows the loss adds a second listwise NLL whose candidate columns are restricted to the rows
of that row's own trajectory, scored in the UNWEIGHTED squared-L2 decision variable — the same
variable the forced-foil instrument uses to pick a content — rather than in the precision-weighted
one the global term uses, so the term optimizes the geometry the measurement actually reads. The
parent's coincidence-class quotient is carried over unchanged: each candidate column's logit is
divided by its class multiplicity and the numerator is the log-sum-exp over the whole positive class,
so a content that occurs several times inside the board counts once and is fully attainable. The term
is averaged over routed reads only, which leaves its total gradient mass comparable to the global
listwise term while concentrating it on the rows where routing decides the answer.

WHY IT SHOULD MOVE comp_ca. comp_ca is native_hit minus swap_stayed on the same board. A model that
keys on which location is being read gives the same answer in both arms and scores exactly zero; the
only thing that survives both arms is carrying a particular content's identity through the moves. The
board-local term penalizes precisely the confusion that produces a zero: at a routed read it demands
that the routed content beat the OTHER contents of that same board in squared L2, and the other
contents of that board are exactly the foils the swapped arm would route there. A prediction that
encodes "something from this board" or "the content this path held before the moves" is unpunished by
the parent's global softmax — every other board's rows are already far away, so the global NLL is
near zero for it and the focal weight then removes what little gradient remains — but it is maximally
punished by the board-local term. I expect the training loss to show a large, slowly-falling
board-local component next to a near-saturated global one, native_hit to rise, and swap_stayed to
fall or hold; and I expect this to be visible more on the deeper windows (d4+) than on d3, since it is
the long chains where an untrained model settles for a board-plausible content.

FALSIFIABLE, and the ways it can fail. (1) If comp_ca does not exceed the parent's +0.0075, the claim
that board-local foils are the missing negatives is wrong for this data. (2) The routed-read detector
could fire mostly on the cvar arms, whose chains are short, and rarely on deep cups chains, in which
case the term trains a shallower skill than the one scored. (3) Concentrating a unit of gradient mass
on ~10% of rows is a real emphasis; if those rows are genuinely unpredictable at this capacity the
term is noise that costs the global term its budget, and the number goes down. (4) The term could
raise native_hit and swap_stayed together — a model that learns "answer with a content from THIS
board" does better on both arms and cancels out — which would show as a flat comp_ca with a healthier
absolute pick rate. No performance claim here is measured: torch is not available in the jail, the
term was written to be correct by construction, and shape/dtype reasoning was done on paper against
the real collate (tgt is [B, maxn, D] per step, rows flatten sequence-contiguous in step order).
Nothing indexes a sequence position or assumes an even length; the term operates only on flattened
command rows. It introduces no parameters. Every mask, threshold and row selection is detached, and
the term is anti-collapse-safe: a constant prediction cannot put the right positive lowest for two
different rows of the same board at once, so the board NLL stays strictly positive.
