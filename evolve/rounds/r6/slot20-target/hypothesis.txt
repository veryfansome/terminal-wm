AXES CHANGED: target only (equal_mass_normal_score_target, replacing confusion_discriminant_metric).
All six other axes are the parent's verbatim.

MECHANISM

The parent re-metrizes the observation space with one global 768x768 linear operator built from
second-moment statistics: whiten by the total moment, then gain up the eigendirections of a
near-neighbour difference moment. Any such map is linear, so it can only reweight DIRECTIONS: it
applies the same stretch to a pair of observations whether they sit on top of each other or on
opposite sides of the cloud.

This candidate changes the axis of adaptation. It applies no direction reweighting at all. Instead
each of the 768 (already per-dimension standardized) coordinates gets its own strictly monotone
piecewise-linear map whose x knots are that coordinate's empirical equal-mass quantiles and whose
y knots are the standard-normal quantiles of the same probability levels — the probability integral
transform, then the normal score, estimated once from a stride-subsample of training targets spread
over the first 200 steps and then frozen. to_obs is the same piecewise-linear function read in the
other direction, so the pair is exact by construction (measured max round-trip error 7e-7 on
standardized-scale inputs, 8e-6 at 25 sigma).

The reason to want this: the local slope of the probability integral transform is proportional to
the DENSITY. Squared error in the transformed space therefore measures the probability mass lying
between the prediction and the truth, not the raw distance — which is the quantity a forced-choice
retrieval actually decides on, namely how many other observations sit between the two. It stretches
the value regions where observations pile up and compresses the regions where they are already far
apart. No linear re-metrization can express that, because it is a reallocation of resolution ALONG
the value axis inside a coordinate rather than across coordinates.

WHY THIS SHOULD MOVE comp_ca

The scored read exposes N contents at N locations and asks which content the model puts at one
location. The candidates being separated are near-duplicates of each other: same cwd, same exit
code, same command shape, differing only in file content (in the training trajectories, e.g. the
16-hex-char body of a .dat file). Two such observations sit inside the dense core of essentially
every embedding coordinate, so their separation is a tiny fraction of the coordinate's range, and a
squared-error objective on raw standardized targets treats getting that separation wrong as almost
no error at all. Meanwhile an ls output versus a cat output are far apart in the tails and already
trivially distinguishable, yet dominate the loss. The transform inverts that budget without
changing the overall scale of the target.

On a synthetic mixture built to have that structure (dominant cluster with a low-rank discrete
content code, other verb clusters, one exactly-repeated plateau class, then per-dimension
standardization), the fitted map amplified mean squared distance between same-cluster
different-content pairs by 13.8x while leaving across-cluster distance unchanged (x1.00), and the
per-dimension target scale stayed at 1.007 against an input scale of 1.007 so the objective's
temperature, ring and margin constants stay calibrated. That is a property of the mechanism on
synthetic data, NOT a performance claim: nothing here has been run against the real harness, and
miniature probes in this project have inverted rank in both directions.

FALSIFIABLE PREDICTIONS

1. The fitted map is materially non-linear: per-dimension segment slopes span close to the imposed
   4x-of-median cap in a large fraction of dimensions. If instead the embedding marginals are near
   Gaussian, the map is near the identity, the candidate is near-identical to the identity-target
   baseline, and comp_ca lands within noise of -0.0075 rather than of the parent.
2. Native pick rate rises relative to the parent while chance does not (the transform is one global
   frozen function applied identically to every window, every candidate and every arm, so it cannot
   move swap_stayed except through the model it trained).
3. comp_ca beats the parent's -0.0000. If it does not, the specific thing to look at is whether
   native rose at all: a rise in native with no rise in comp_ca would say the extra resolution
   bought generic retrieval and not content tracking, which is the distinction this objective exists
   to make.

WHY NO OTHER AXIS CHANGES

The genome's engine is a copy path: the arch adds retrieved raw observation embeddings into the
command-position prediction, and the head adds a routed copy through a per-dimension out_scale. A
target transform breaks the alignment of that copy — the copied vector is no longer the tensor being
predicted. This transform is DIAGONAL, one scalar function per coordinate, and the arch already
applies a per-dimension state-conditioned affine to exactly that prediction
(pred_cmd * (1 + g_sys) + b_sys, both [.., D]), which can locally linearize a per-coordinate monotone
warp around the copied value. A full 768x768 rotation, which is what the parent's map contains,
cannot be re-expressed by that FiLM at all. So this transform needs less arch help than the parent's
did, and I changed no arch, head, objective, optim, batcher or stream code.

WHAT I BUILT FIRST AND REJECTED

I first built and tested a trainable unit-determinant linear metric (bounded per-dimension log-gains
plus a Householder-orthonormal K-dimensional subspace gain, all log-gains sharing one offset so
det F = 1 exactly, so gradient could only REALLOCATE precision, never shrink the problem). It is
exactly invertible and every parameter receives gradient and reaches the read. It is also degenerate:
jointly descending on the model and on the metric is winner-take-all, because the metric's cheapest
move is to point at whatever the model already predicts best, which then gets predicted better still.
On a synthetic world with one large, fully predictable nuisance direction, the descent version
amplified that direction ~4x versus a random direction and dropped 64-way forced choice from 0.799 to
0.783/0.729/0.787 over three seeds. Reversing the gradient (a unit-volume ADVERSARIAL metric, a
directional DRO) correctly suppressed the nuisance but was worse still, 0.799 -> 0.354/0.308/0.313,
because it reweights by unpredictability rather than by discriminability. Both failures share one
cause: the loss gradient ranks directions by how well the model does on them, which is not what the
retrieval decides on. That is why the mechanism I shipped is estimated from the DATA's own marginal
distribution with no model in the loop and no trainable parameters at all.

RISKS

- Biggest: the copy path. If the genome's compositional ability lives entirely in adding a retrieved
  raw observation embedding into the prediction, then warping the target degrades it, and the
  per-dimension FiLM argument above is an argument, not a measurement.
- The knots come from the training split and are applied to windows held out by image. A monotone
  map extrapolates linearly outside its knots and cannot become non-monotone, so shift degrades the
  map gracefully rather than breaking it, but the equal-mass property is only exact in-distribution.
- The target space changes abruptly at step 200 (identity before, warped after). The optimizer's
  warmup ends around then, so there is LR available to re-converge, but it is a discontinuity.
- Amplifying the dense core also amplifies whatever encoder noise lives there. The slope cap
  (4x the dimension's median slope), the per-dimension unit-variance calibration and the
  identity fallback for dimensions with no spread bound this, but do not remove it.

COST AND SAFETY

No trainable parameters (the parent also has none), so nothing new to reach. One searchsorted plus
four gathers per call: measured 3.4 ms per training call for 1400x768 on MPS and 6 ms on CPU, and one
0.3 s fit. Fits and runs on cpu, mps and any device torch.searchsorted supports. Fails closed: any
exception, non-finite knot or non-monotone knot vector during the fit sets a dead flag and both
directions stay exactly the identity for the rest of the run.
