AXES CHANGED: target only (rank_gaussianized_contrast_metric_target). arch, batcher, head,
objective, optim and stream are the parent's, verbatim. The mechanism is a pure re-metrization of
the space the loss is computed in; it needs no other axis, and the parent's objective is already
the ideal consumer for it, so touching a second axis would only add a confound.

WHAT IT IS. One full cycle of iterative Gaussianization (Laparra, Camps-Valls & Malo, "Iterative
Gaussianization: from ICA to random rotations", IEEE Trans. Neural Networks 2011): a per-coordinate
marginal Gaussianization followed by a linear transform of the Gaussianized coordinates. Stage one
is the probability integral transform of the crossover partner: each of the 768 standardized
observation dimensions gets its own strictly monotone piecewise-linear map from that dimension's
empirical equal-mass quantiles to the standard-normal quantiles of the same probability levels,
fitted once from a stride subsample over the first ~200 steps and frozen. Stage two is the parent's
map, Cov_contrast^(q/2) Cov_total^(-a/2), with contrast pairs drawn from mutually nearest
observations inside the same trajectory — but estimated and applied entirely in the coordinates
stage one produces. The inverse is the linear inverse assembled from the same eigenpairs followed by
the same knots read backwards, an exact algebraic inverse of the composite, so to_obs reconstructs
and reads only frozen buffers.

WHY THIS ORDER IS THE ACTUAL CROSSOVER. Stacking the two parents in the parent's order (linear, then
marginal) would be close to vacuous: each output coordinate of a dense 768-dimensional linear mix is
a sum of many weakly dependent terms, so its marginal is already near-Gaussian and the per-dimension
transform would come out nearly affine. In the order used here the two stages genuinely compose.
The rank transform acts where the non-Gaussianity actually lives, in the frozen encoder's own
coordinates, which have dense cores and long tails; and the linear stage is then estimated on
coordinates that are standard normal by construction, which means its total second moment IS the
normal-scores (Gaussian rank) correlation matrix and its contrast neighbours are chosen by
rank-space distance. That repairs a specific weakness of the parent rather than merely adding to it:
the parent's eigenbasis comes from a raw second moment, where a handful of large-norm rows can drag
the leading directions, whereas a rank correlation is a bounded-influence estimator — no single row
can contribute more than a bounded amount to any entry.

WHY IT SHOULD MOVE comp_ca. The differential cancels anything keyed on which location is read: both
arms present the same read token, so a location-keyed predictor scores exactly zero per window. What
survives is whether the model resolves WHICH CONTENT now sits there, and at the read that is a
forced choice among observations of the same board that differ only in file content — same cwd, same
exit code, same output shape. Those observations sit in the dense core of every encoder dimension.
After the rank transform, squared error between two such observations measures the probability MASS
between them rather than the raw distance, so their tiny separations are amplified and already
trivially distinguishable observations in the tails are compressed; after the linear stage, that
amplified resolution is further concentrated on the directions along which co-occurring confusable
observations actually differ. The parent's objective consumes this directly: its hard-negative ring,
duplicate pooling and temperature are all fractions of the mean in-batch distance in target space, so
reshaping that geometry retunes which negatives are hard and moves gradient budget off directions
that only separate observations the retrieval was never going to confuse.

WHAT I EXPECT TO SEE (all performance claims unmeasured — I cannot run the harness from the jail).
Primary: comp_ca above the parent's +0.0112. Mechanistic checks that make this falsifiable rather
than a bet on a number: (1) the gain should appear as a higher native hit rate with swapped-held
roughly unchanged, because the mechanism acts on content discrimination and touches nothing that
could encode where in the move order a content sits; if the improvement instead shows up as
swapped-follows, that is chain-position leaning and this hypothesis is wrong even if the score rises.
(2) If the fitted knots come out close to affine — i.e. the encoder's marginals are already near
Gaussian — the composite degenerates to the parent and the score should reproduce the parent's
within seed noise; that is the null this design is exposed to. (3) Because the map is one global
function of value, identical for every window, and reads neither z_prev nor any observation, it
cannot behave differently at the position whose observation is withheld.

SCHEDULE, AND WHY IT CHANGED. The two stages have to be staged in time: knots fitted while the
linear map is still being rebuilt would describe a space that then moves under them. So the marginal
is fitted and frozen by call ~200 (with a hard deadline at 600 calls so it can never stall), the
second moments are accumulated only afterwards, the linear exponents ramp from call ~350 and the
whole composite freezes by ~1150 — earlier than the parent's 1500 — so the model still trains most
of its budget against a stationary target.

COST AND FAILURE MODES. Added per-step cost is one batched searchsorted plus four gathers over
[768, rows] under no_grad, and one 64x768 host copy on each of the first 200 steps; the one-time
knot fit sorts at most 12288x768 float32 on CPU; the linear stage does seven float64 eigendecom-
positions of 768x768, fewer than the parent's eight. Every failure path degrades rather than breaks:
identity until the knots are fitted; identity marginal if the fitted knots fail the strict-
monotonicity check, in which case the candidate is exactly the parent with a shorter schedule; the
pure rank map if training ends before the linear stage warms; and the linear stage keeps the parent's
finiteness guards so a bad rebuild leaves the previous map in place. The impl introduces no trainable
parameters at all — only buffers — so there is no parameter that could fail to receive gradient. The
single biggest risk is the null in check (2): if the encoder's per-dimension marginals are already
close to normal, the rank stage is nearly the identity and this is the parent with a different
schedule.
