Every other axis keeps the parent's impl and params verbatim. No other axis needs to move: the
new mechanism adds no parameters, does not touch forward, and reads the prediction the harness

WHAT THE PARENT DOES, AND THE HOLE IN IT

The parent's single arm mines (pre, mutating-cmd, post) triples whose three positions are mutually
near in command-embedding cosine, and regresses the shared latent-transition operator onto the
post observation with a change-weighted cosine+MSE. Its mining weight is
  w = sim(k,i) * sim(k,j) * mean((obs_i - obs_j)^2),
kept only when mean((obs_i - obs_j)^2) >= change_floor and w > 0. So a span across which the
observation did NOT change contributes exactly zero, structurally.

Checked against evolve/jail_data/train_sample.jsonl: the trajectories are built as
  cat P_1 ... cat P_m        (contents exposed at locations, each read a distinct content)
  mv A B; mv B C; ...        (a silent chain, output "" for every step)
  cat P_final                (a read whose content is one of the exposed ones, unchanged)
render_obs is "cwd=<cwd> exit=<code>\n<output>", so the exposure read and the post-chain read of
a routed content produce byte-identical observation text, hence (up to encoder fp noise)
identical embeddings; and every silent step in a row produces one and the same observation text.
The routed read is therefore exactly a zero-change span -- precisely the case the parent's
change_floor deletes. The parent trains the operator only where content was rewritten, and never
where content was carried. That is the hole this candidate fills.

THE MECHANISM ADDED (one arm, train-time only, no new parameters)

A second arm supervises the model's OWN next-observation prediction at exactly the reads a silent
chain routed, and supervises it discriminatively rather than by regression.

Mining, from embedding geometry alone (no strings, no_grad), per row:
  1. dup[a,b] = cos(unit(obs_a), unit(obs_b)) >= dup_thresh (0.98).
  2. The duplicate cluster with the largest membership is the row's silent/mutating set (all
     silent steps share one observation text; contents recur at most a couple of times). Rows
     whose largest cluster is smaller than silent_min (3) contribute nothing.
  3. A window is a pair of NON-silent duplicates i < j with at least min_chain silent steps
     strictly between them. depth = #silent in (i, j) is the chain length; for each j the
     earliest such i is taken, which is the original exposure and the deepest chain.
  4. Candidates are the first-occurrence representatives of the row's non-silent duplicate
     classes at positions strictly before j -- i.e. the other contents already exposed on the
     same board. The positive is the exposure vector at i (identical to obs_j, and at a strictly
     earlier position), so the entire candidate set lies in the causal prefix of the read.

Loss at the command position j, over the C nearest-in-time candidates (C = 8, positive always
included):
  logits_c = -mean_d (pred_j - cand_c)^2 / tau      (tau = 0.25)
  L_win    = -log softmax(logits)_positive
  L        = sum_w  (depth_w^depth_pow / sum depth^depth_pow) * L_win,   depth capped at 8
folded in with weight nce_weight (0.25) under the parent's existing smoothstep ramp and
aux_weight. The parent's transition-consistency arm is kept verbatim and still runs
(legacy_weight = 1.0); this is an addition, not a replacement, so the comparison against the
parent isolates the new term.

WHY THIS SHOULD MOVE comp_ca

comp_ca = mean over windows of (native_hit - swap_stayed). A predictor that keys on the location
being read cancels exactly; what survives is carrying a particular content's identity through the
chain of moves. The scored decision at a read is an N-way forced-foil pick by squared distance
against confusable same-verb observations. Three specific reasons this arm should raise the
differential:

1. Gradient placement. The main objective spends its listwise pressure uniformly over every
   command position in the batch, and the large majority of those positions have the trivial
   silent observation as their target. The mined windows are ~3 per trajectory (77 across the 24
   sampled rows), and they are exactly the positions the metric scores. This concentrates
   gradient by roughly an order of magnitude onto the routed reads without changing the loss the
   rest of the sequence sees.

2. Negatives that are the board, not the batch. The confusables at scoring time are the other
   contents sitting on the same system -- same file format, same canary-line shape, different
   identity. In-batch negatives are drawn across images and content families; the ring in the
   objective also deliberately down-weights near-identical targets. Here the negatives are the
   other exposed contents of the same board, in the eval's own squared-L2 decision variable. An
   L2 regression's gradient on near-parallel targets is dominated by their shared component; a
   softmax over -d^2/tau is dominated by the component that separates them. That is the
   difference between "be near a config file" and "be this config file".

3. Depth weighting. Mined depth on the sample is mean 3.7, max 12, which is the same regime as
   the scored slice (n=89: d3 60, d4+ 29). Weighting each window by its chain depth puts the
   pressure on the long chains, where a shallow heuristic and genuine tracking diverge.

Nothing in the arm mentions position in the move order: the positive is defined by content
identity and the negatives by co-presence on the board, so the pressure is toward the component
that survives both arms of the paired difference rather than toward the first-mover lookup that
the frozen slice happens to reward.

WHAT I EXPECT TO SEE (all unmeasured -- no real training run is possible from the jail)

comp_ca above the parent's +0.0337, driven by native_hit rising more than swap_stayed. If the arm
works, the pick rate against the analytic ceiling should also rise, and shortcut_leaning should
not: the arm supplies no positional signal. A null result that would falsify the mechanism is
comp_ca unchanged with the gate reading unchanged -- that would say the discriminative pressure
at routed reads is already saturated by the objective's ring.

MEASURED FACTS ABOUT THE IMPLEMENTATION (from the jail, on synthetic per-text embeddings that
reproduce the real duplicate structure exactly; these are mechanical checks, not performance
claims): the miner recovers 77 windows over the 24 sampled trajectories, identical to the count
obtained by clustering the rendered observation TEXT directly, and the largest-cluster rule
identified a subset of the truly silent steps in all 24 rows; mean depth 3.69, max 12; mean 7.1
candidates per window; zero windows whose positive or whose candidates lie at or after the read
position; the arm alone produces gradient on 109 parameters including the trunk attention, the
readout, the transition readout and the composition renderer; per-step wall clock is +7% over the
parent on CPU at bs=32.

RISKS AND GUARDS

- The prediction is read off a forward hook on the net, so the arm costs no second forward. The
  hook stashes only when grad is enabled and the module is training, the stash is cleared on
  every path out of aux_loss, and if the stash is missing or shape-mismatched the arm recomputes
  the forward itself rather than scoring the wrong batch. The hook stores into a plain dict held
  outside the module graph, so there is no parent-child module cycle and forward is not
  re-pointed.
- The arm compares the prediction to raw observation vectors, which is correct for an identity
  target. It disables itself when the genome installs a learned target module; a pure non-identity
  target chunk would be a mis-pairing and is not detectable from the head's inputs.
- Silent-set identification is a heuristic over embedding duplicates. If a row's largest duplicate
  cluster is not the silent set the arm mines nothing useful for that row (it needs an intervening
  member of that cluster), so the failure mode is a dropped window rather than a wrong label.
- Odd-length layouts: aux_loss runs only on training collates, refuses any batch failing the
  interleave layout check, and all indexing is derived from cmd_mask's width rather than assuming
  a relation between L and the number of steps.
