AXES CHANGED: batcher only (r6_sysblock_hardneg_curriculum -> r7_reread_yield_madow_pps).
Every other axis keeps the parent's impl and params verbatim. The mechanism is entirely a
question of which sequences co-occur in a batch and how often each is drawn; it needs no
change to the loss, the architecture, the optimizer, the target, the stream or the head,
and touching any of those would confound the reading.

WHAT THE PARENT DOES AND WHY I DID NOT KEEP IT AS IS
The parent anneals from uniform to 75% of the batch drawn, with replacement, from one
randomly chosen system image. It treats every trajectory in that image as interchangeable
and it treats a batch as a lottery. Two things follow. First, the multinomial draw inside a
single image's pool collides with itself: measured on a realistic split at bs=64 it returns
about 61 distinct sequences out of 64, so roughly three slots per batch are spent on a
sequence already present, and a duplicated sequence contributes rows that the paired
objective folds into an exact equivalence class and downweights. Second, and much more
important, trajectories are NOT interchangeable for this objective.

THE PROPERTY THE PARENT IGNORES, MEASURED ON REAL TRAJECTORIES
The paired objective r6_occupancy_transport_name_decoy does not weight rows uniformly. It
gives four times the occupancy weight to rows it calls "repeat": a row whose target exactly
duplicates an earlier row of the SAME trajectory issued under a DIFFERENT command, with a
duplicate set of at most four. That is the post-chain re-read - the read of a location that
a move filled - and it is the row whose geometry matches what the score actually asks about.
The decoy hinge is narrower still: it fires only on such a row when at least two earlier
rows of the trajectory carry different content and one earlier command stands out in
similarity, which under the parent's stream means an earlier read whose path shares atoms
with the re-read's path - the name's former occupant, present in the same trajectory.
I counted both predicates directly on the real trajectories in evolve/jail_data. The count
of re-read rows per trajectory runs from 1 to 6 (mean 2.9, sd 1.3) and the count of
decoy-bearing re-reads from 1 to 5. A sixfold spread, and the parent samples it flat. The
decoy term is also normalized by its own gate mass and returns nothing at all when no row is
gated, so the number of gated rows in a batch sets the variance of exactly the gradient that
teaches "do not answer with what this NAME used to hold" - the one term in the loss aimed at
the shortcut the score is built to cancel.

THE MECHANISM
Two changes serving one idea: give each sequence a size measure counting the rows that carry
the transport signal, and then draw the batch as an exact design instead of a lottery.

(1) Size measure. Per sequence, from its own standardized observation embeddings and its own
command strings: size = 1 + w_reread * (re-read rows) + w_decoy * (decoy-bearing re-reads),
using the objective's own predicates rather than a proxy for them. Duplicate detection is
exact, not approximate: two identical rendered observations produce identical embeddings, so
their distance is exactly zero under any linear projection, which is why a variance-
preserving Gaussian projection to 96 dims can cut the per-sequence distance matrices to a
fraction of their cost without risking the equality test. On the real trajectories the
projected measure reproduces the full-space counts with zero error.

(2) Madow systematic probability-proportional-to-size sampling, imported from survey
sampling (Madow 1949; the same family as the balanced-sampling cube method). Inclusion
probabilities are set proportional to size^gamma, scaled to sum to exactly the batch size,
and capped at one by the standard redistribute-the-excess fixpoint. The frame is freshly
permuted, the probabilities are accumulated, and one uniform start u selects the units whose
cumulative intervals contain u, u+1, ..., u+bs-1. This draws without replacement, realizes
each unit's inclusion probability exactly rather than in expectation over repeated
multinomial trials, and holds the count at exactly bs with zero variance. gamma ramps from
zero on the same schedule as the hard fraction, so the opening of training is the plain
distribution and the tilt arrives with the blocking. The parent's annealed same-image
concentration is kept verbatim in structure as the first stage - images are chosen
proportional to their aggregate size, the hard share is drawn systematically inside them -
and the remaining slots are drawn systematically from everything not already selected, so
the batch is fully distinct end to end.

WHAT I EXPECT TO SEE, AND WHAT WOULD FALSIFY IT
Measured on a realistic split (n=2400, bs=64, 400 steps) against both the parent's scheme
and plain uniform: re-read rows per batch rise from 187 to 224 (+20%), decoy-bearing rows
from 157 to 191 (+21%), distinct sequences per batch from 60.7 to exactly 64.0, while late-
training same-image concentration is unchanged (49.8 vs 50.2 slots of 64). Those are counts
of the batch's composition, measured, not a claim about the score. The score claim is
downstream and UNMEASURED: I expect comp_ca to rise because a larger and less variable share
of each step's gradient comes from the rows the objective upweights and the decoy hinge
gates, which is the part of the loss that pushes the model to answer a re-read with what the
chain routed there rather than with what the name used to hold. Nothing here can move the
score by keying on chain position: the batcher never sees the eval, and the size measure is a
per-sequence count that is identical under any relabeling of which content moves where.
The hypothesis is falsified if comp_ca does not move despite the composition shift; that
would say the transport terms were never gradient-starved and the batcher axis is spent on
this objective. It is falsified in the other direction, and worse, if comp_ca moves while the
capability gate falls, which would mean the tilt is buying the differential by narrowing what
the model trains on rather than by teaching transport.

COST AND CORRECTNESS
Setup is one projection pass over all rows plus one small distance matrix per sequence:
0.23 s at n=2400 and 0.99 s at n=10080, one time. Per-step cost is 0.28 ms at n=10080, which
over a 4000-step budget is about one second in total. The batcher introduces no parameters,
so there is nothing for a reachability gate to reach; it reads fit and never mutates it,
owns two private generators and touches no global RNG, and is deterministic given the seed
(verified by replaying 400 steps from a second construction). Contract, bounds and length
were exercised at bs=33 and bs=64, on splits of size 2, 5, 64, 65, 480, 2400 and 10080, and
under degenerate settings (hard_frac_max=0, gamma_max=0 and 4.0, eq_frac=1.0, proj_dim=8,
n_block_images=3). It falls back to uniform when the batch is not smaller than the split or
no size measure can be built, and the image stage is skipped when there are fewer than two
images.
