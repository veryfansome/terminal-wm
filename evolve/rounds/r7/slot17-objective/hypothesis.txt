MECHANISM: a command-stratified conditional partial likelihood, imported from conditional maximum
likelihood in discrete choice / panel econometrics (McFadden's conditional logit, the Rasch
conditional likelihood, and the Cox partial likelihood). Those estimators eliminate a per-group
nuisance parameter not by modelling it but by conditioning on a statistic that makes it drop out of
the likelihood: within a group, the group effect multiplies every alternative equally and cancels
from the ratio. Here the nuisance is exactly the thing the metric already cancels — the command.

Rows in the flattened training batch are partitioned into COHORTS of numerically equal causal
command embeddings (the same command string, cheap relative-threshold equality on ctx["cmd"],
1e-3 of the batch's mean pairwise distance — the tolerance an archived impl already uses for exact
duplicate detection in this same standardized space). The primary loss term is a multi-positive
listwise likelihood whose DENOMINATOR IS RESTRICTED TO THE ANCHOR'S OWN COHORT. Every candidate in
that denominator answers the identical question, so a predictor of the form pred = f(cmd) emits
the same logit row for every member of the cohort: it is tied across the cohort and cannot make
row i prefer its own answer while row i' prefers a different one. Its achievable value is the
cohort floor, which is a constant of the batch. That is the training-side analogue of the way
comp_ca cancels location-keying — the loss refuses to pay for the shortcut instead of merely
out-weighting it. Measured on synthetic batches with this structure, the BEST command-only
predictor (the per-cohort mean target) scores 3.963 on the conditional term versus 3.971 for a
random predictor and 0.213 for the oracle: the command channel buys essentially nothing, and the
whole 3.75-nat gap is reachable only through the trajectory history.

Two refinements pick out the compositional rows rather than the merely-unpredictable ones.
(1) Inside a cohort, distinct-target candidates drawn from the anchor's OWN trajectory carry an
additive logit bonus (+4). Where a command was issued twice in one trajectory and returned
different content — a read of a location before and after a silent move, which is 6.6% of the rows
in the jail's real trajectory sample and the exact shape of the scored window — the conditional
problem collapses onto that pair, so the gradient is "is the old content or the new content there
now", not "which of sixty systems am I on". The bonus is applied to negatives only, so rows with no
same-trajectory alternative keep an ordinary cohort softmax rather than becoming trivially easy.
(2) Row mass is raised for rows whose answer already occurred earlier in their own trajectory
(retrievable, x2) and for rows that have a same-trajectory same-command alternative (x5). This is
deliberately NOT focal weighting: much of the cross-system content is a random per-image hash that
was never observed in the prefix and is irreducible, and focal loss would pour the conditional
gradient into exactly that noise. Focal weighting is kept only on the auxiliary global term.

The rest is inherited from the parent's lineage: exact-target equivalence classes pooled
multi-positively with inverse-class mass, per-dimension precision weighting, squared-L2 distances
in the eval's own decision variable, a repulsion hinge with a margin, and an untouched raw MSE
anchor so a constant prediction cannot minimize the loss (measured: 6.05 for the best constant
versus 0.032 for the oracle). Two things from the parent are DROPPED: the confusability ring is
gone — negatives are now selected by command identity rather than by target proximity, which is
what the probe's forced choice actually presents — and the full-batch listwise term survives only
at weight 0.3, as a geometry calibrator for the N-way choice, because at full weight it is the term
a command-keyer can still profit from.

AXES CHANGED and why they change together (operator is CROSSOVER):
- objective -> r7_command_stratified_conditional_likelihood (new; the mechanism above).
- arch -> r6_capacity_ledger_transport, head -> r4_chainlink_route_transport, batcher ->
  r20_depthtilted_sysblock_curriculum: the partner's half of the cross, taken as the co-designed
  unit it is. The conditional term supplies a gradient that ONLY a content-routing computation can
  earn; it needs a parameterization in which "which earlier observation is at this location now" is
  an expressible choice. The capacity ledger is exactly that — its prediction at a command position
  is a link-weighted combination of earlier observation embeddings, depleted as content is moved
  away — and the chainlink head supervises that routing distribution directly. The depth-tilted
  curriculum comes with them because the boost the objective puts on same-trajectory alternatives
  only bites when deep move chains are present in the batch. Pairing the new objective with the
  parent's arch instead would leave the strongest gradient it produces with nothing to move: the
  parent scores +0.0000, which is the value the metric returns structurally for a pure
  location-keyer, so the parent's stack is sitting on the exact fixed point this objective is built
  to make unprofitable.
- target, stream, optim: unchanged, and identical in both halves of the cross. The objective's
  temperature, margin and both equality thresholds are fractions of the batch's mean off-diagonal
  target distance precisely so it stays calibrated while within_sequence_contrast_equalizer
  rescales the target space during training.

EXPECTATION (falsifiable): comp_ca rises above the parent's +0.0000, and above the +0.0112 floor.
The specific prediction that distinguishes this mechanism from a lucky reroll: because the
conditional term is tied across a cohort, a shortcut that keys on the read command cannot be the
source of any gain, so a positive comp_ca should come with a low private.shortcut_leaning and the
gain should concentrate in the d4+ windows rather than d3. If comp_ca moves but the leaning
diagnostic moves with it, the mechanism is not what produced the number. The failure mode I would
expect if the hypothesis is wrong is comp_ca near zero with a healthy loss curve: that would mean
the cohorts are real and the conditional gap is being closed by per-image prefix identity — routing
the batch's system identity rather than a particular content — which cohort restriction alone does
not forbid.

UNMEASURED: every number above is either from the jail's real trajectory sample (cohort and
repeat-command frequencies) or from synthetic batches with the same shape (the command-only floor,
the anti-collapse gap, cost). No claim about comp_ca has been measured. Cost: forward+backward at
n=2048 rows is ~78 ms on CPU against ~69 ms for the parent objective on the same batch, i.e. one
extra n-by-n command distance matrix on top of the two the parent already forms; it should not
threaten the fixed step budget.
