AXES CHANGED: objective only (r22_exact_target_equivalence_quotient ->
r7_paired_difference_alignment). arch, optim, target, batcher, stream and head are the parent's
verbatim. The mechanism is a pure loss-shaping term over (pred, tgt) rows, so it needs no
collaborating change anywhere else: it introduces no parameters, no new tensors in the batch, and
no assumption about the token layout. Touching another axis would have been an unjustified
liability.

THE MECHANISM. Keep the whole r22 backbone (exact-target equivalence classes, occurrence measure
1/class_size, per-dimension precision weighting, class-balanced MSE anchor, focal listwise
class-NLL, confusability-ring importance and the gated repulsion hinge) and ADD one term: a paired
difference-alignment hinge over rows that are near each other in the flattened cmd-position order.

Take two rows i and j with numerically distinct targets. The matched-versus-swapped assignment
cost in the eval's own decision variable is

    s_ij = d2(i,i) + d2(j,j) - d2(i,j) - d2(j,i)

and it collapses algebraically to exactly

    s_ij = -2 (p_i - p_j) . (t_i - t_j) / D.

Every squared-norm term cancels. So "the matched assignment must beat the swapped assignment" is
identical to "the prediction difference must have positive projection on the target difference".
I score that projection as a scale-free regression coefficient,

    alpha_ij = (p_i - p_j).(t_i - t_j) / max( ||t_i - t_j||^2 , 0.1 * mean_offdiag ),

and penalise it with a smooth hinge, tau * softplus((0.75 - alpha_ij)/tau), tau = 0.25. The
denominator clamp keeps a near-duplicate-but-distinct pair from dividing by a vanishing number
while still leaving those pairs the hardest ones. Pairs are restricted to |i - j| <= 16 (rows of
one trajectory are contiguous in the flattened order, so this is a within-board neighbourhood,
which is where the sibling contents of a window live) and weighted by the r22 occurrence measure
1/class_i * 1/class_j, so the very large exact-equal class of silent commands -- every mv, every
redirect, whose rendered observation is the identical "cwd=... exit=0" string, verified in
evolve/jail_data/train_sample.jsonl -- contributes essentially nothing and content-versus-content
pairs carry the mass. Everything except alpha's numerator is detached. Cost is one extra n-by-n
elementwise pass; the pred/tgt matmul is reused rather than recomputed, so the step-time increase
should be small (unmeasured).

WHY IT SHOULD MOVE comp_ca. Two properties, neither of which the parent has.

(1) It is blind to the shared component of a pair of predictions. Any part of p_i and p_j that is
common to both -- the conditional mean of "a config-file-shaped blob", the generic empty-output
direction, and in particular anything keyed on the read location alone -- cancels out of
(p_i - p_j) exactly, not on average. So the entire gradient of this term is spent on the direction
that separates the two contents. Squared-error and the listwise NLL both spend most of their
gradient budget on the common direction, because that is where the mass of the residual is.
comp_ca is itself a paired difference in which everything common to the two arms cancels, so this
term optimises the same shape of quantity at training time that the metric measures at scoring
time.

(2) It attacks shrinkage. A model minimising expected squared error predicts a shrunk conditional
mean; shrinkage is invisible to MSE ranking but fatal to a nearest-neighbour readout among N
similar in-window contents, because the shrunk predictions of two different reads sit closer to
each other than to their respective contents. alpha_ij is exactly the shrinkage coefficient of the
pair, and the hinge at 0.75 demands the prediction difference recover three quarters of the target
difference. The hinge saturates: once a pair is separated there is no further pressure, and
inflating the prediction norm buys nothing, so it cannot be gamed by scaling.

WHAT I EXPECT TO SEE. Native pick rate on the eligible windows should rise, driven by reads whose
sibling contents are close in encoder space; swap_stayed should fall or hold, because a model that
gives two in-window reads the same answer is now penalised directly and such a model is exactly one
whose native and swapped predictions coincide. The expected signature is a higher comp_ca with the
paired difference, not the absolute distance, doing the work. Absolute latent MSE may get slightly
WORSE while comp_ca improves -- that dissociation is the falsifiable prediction. If comp_ca does
not move but MSE does, the mechanism is inert; if both degrade, the de-shrinkage pressure was too
strong.

STATUS OF PERFORMANCE CLAIMS. Unmeasured. I cannot run the harness from the jail; the algebraic
identity s_ij = -2 (p_i - p_j).(t_i - t_j)/D is verified on paper, and the class/weight structure
was checked against the real trajectories in evolve/jail_data/train_sample.jsonl, but nothing here
has been trained.

BIGGEST RISK. The band is a proxy for "same board": rows are contiguous per sequence in the
flattened cmd-position order, but I cannot see sequence boundaries from inside the objective, so a
band that straddles a boundary mixes in cross-system pairs. Those pairs are easy, the hinge
saturates on them and they contribute no gradient, but they do enter the normaliser and dilute the
term. The second risk is the de-shrinkage target itself: 0.75 is a guess, and a value that is too
aggressive would trade absolute accuracy for separation and could lower the native pick rate faster
than it lowers swap_stayed.
