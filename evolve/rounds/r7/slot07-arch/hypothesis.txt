THE CLAIM I AM ATTACKING

Every prior transport arch in my slot's evidence (parent r18, r25, r23, r5) resolves a move the
same way: learn one or more linear/GELU readouts off the raw command embedding and hope one of
them lands on the SOURCE path and a different one lands on the DESTINATION path. r25 has three
role heads sharing a trunk; r23 has addr_src and addr_dst of identical shape; r5 slices two fixed
coordinate windows out of the raw token. All of them are trying to make one readout select "the
first path argument" and another select "the second".

The observations are mean-pooled frozen encoder embeddings. Mean pooling over token positions is
permutation-symmetric in the tokens it pools, so to first order the embedding of `mv A B` is a
BAG: verb mass plus the token mass of A plus the token mass of B, with the A/B distinction
surviving only through second-order contextual effects in the encoder's attention. A linear map
of a bag is symmetric in the two summands. My hypothesis is that this near-symmetry is why the
separate-destination-head family has not converted into differential score: the head being asked
to isolate the destination is being asked to break a symmetry that the pooled representation
mostly does not carry.

THE MECHANISM

Bag-difference address arithmetic. One shared linear map A is applied to every raw command
embedding. Subtracting a learned per-role offset vector gives either

    read address     s(P)          for a one-argument command   (`cat P`)
    joint address    s(X) + s(Y)   for a two-argument command   (`mv X Y`)

The model never learns a destination readout. Instead the store resolves the source from STATE:
occupancy-biased attention finds which currently-occupied slot's address the joint address
overlaps (logits = cos(joint, key)/tau + kappa*log(occ)), and the destination is then DERIVED by
arithmetic,

    a_dst = unit( rho * joint - eta * a_src + b )

with rho, eta scalars and b a bias vector, all learned. This breaks the A/B symmetry using
something the pooled embedding genuinely does carry — which of the two paths already holds
content — rather than something it mostly does not.

Slots are addressed DNC-style (Graves et al. 2016, Nature): a write blends content matching
(cosine against slot keys, occupancy-biased) with a usage-sorted free-list allocation
(cumprod of ascending usage), gated by whether any occupied slot actually matches. Slot keys move
toward the address they were written at, so a path seen for the first time claims a free slot and
is content-addressable ever after. Occupancy is asserted on write and erased at the resolved
source on a move.

The content written at the destination is the content standing at the resolved source, passed
through the shared command-conditioned affine transition operator f(s,cmd) = s*(1+gamma) + beta
that the head axis supervises via transition_from_emb (kept, same signature). The store read is
blended with the previous-observation channel and injected at command positions through an
identity-initialised (D,D) tr_read readout under a sigmoid gate.

WHY THIS SHOULD MOVE THE OBJECTIVE

The objective is a paired difference between the native chain and a chain in which two contents
exchange their move positions. A model that keys on the location being read predicts identically
in both arms and contributes exactly zero. This store cannot do that: its answer at a read is
whatever content the chain of derived destinations deposited at that address. In a chain
A -> B -> C, the first move resolves source A (occupied), derives B, and deposits A's content
there with B now occupied; the second move's joint is s(B) + s(C), resolves source B because B is
now the occupied one, and derives C = joint - s(B). Identity is carried, not move-order position:
nothing in the store reads step index, and the source is chosen by occupancy, not by recency or
by being first. That is the thing the metric says survives both arms.

WHAT I EXPECT TO SEE

comp_ca above the parent's -0.0112, driven by native_hit at depth 3 and depth 4+ rising while
swap_stayed falls, with the gap widening rather than shrinking with depth (a first-mover lookup
has no depth signature; identity carrying does). I expect shortcut_leaning to stay low precisely
because no step index enters the store. All of this is UNMEASURED — I could not run the harness
or the metric from the jail.

WHAT I DID VERIFY MECHANICALLY, in the jail, on synthetic addresses

- Shapes and finiteness for L in {1,2,3,5,9,12,57}, including odd L where the final command has
  no following observation; the store loop runs n_cmd=(L+1)//2 steps and the last step's
  observation write is gated to exactly zero rather than taking a different branch.
- Every named parameter receives non-zero gradient from a loss on pred alone (the transition
  MLP's output layer is initialised small rather than zero for exactly this reason: a zero-init
  tr_out leaves tr_in and tr_mut_gate with identically zero gradient at init).
- Causality: perturbing the observation at position 7 leaves the predictions at positions 0..6
  bit-identical and changes positions 7 onward.
- Transport: with the address map set to read planted signatures, a read after 1, 2 and 3 silent
  hops returns the originally exposed content at cosine 0.996-0.998 with attention sharpness
  ~0.99, untouched locations return their own content, and the derived destination is never
  supplied from outside. This is a test of the arithmetic, not a performance claim.
- Speed: 3 forward+backward steps at B=64, L=57 in 1.5 s on CPU, same order as the parent's
  sequential loop; the per-step state is one [B, slots, 768] fused update rather than two.

BIGGEST RISK

The bag additivity is an approximation. The encoder is a transformer, not a bag-of-words model,
and the pooled vector of `mv A B` is only approximately s(A) + s(B) after the shared map; if the
residual non-additivity is large, `joint - a_src` will not land near s(B) and the derived
destination degenerates into a noisy address that the free-list keeps allocating fresh slots for.
The learned rho, eta and b can absorb a global affine mismatch but not a content-dependent one.
A secondary risk is that the source resolution is occupancy-driven, so an exposed-but-never-moved
distractor whose signature happens to overlap the joint address can be picked as the source; at
key_d=64 the overlap between unrelated path signatures was small in the synthetic check, but the
real signatures are not random and share long common prefixes such as /tmp/w/cups/.
