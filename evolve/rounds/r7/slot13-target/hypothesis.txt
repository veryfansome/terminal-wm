AXES CHANGED: target only (r6_spectral_equalized_target -> r7_local_contrast_expanding_target).
The other six axes are the parent's, verbatim. Nothing else needs to move: the mechanism is a
change to which subspace of the regression target the training loss is forced to pay for, and it
is expressed entirely inside the target chunk's own transform and regulariser.

WHAT THE PICK ACTUALLY DEPENDS ON

The scored read chooses between candidate observation embeddings by squared distance to the
prediction mapped back through to_obs. For two candidates c1 and c2,

    ||y - c1||^2 - ||y - c2||^2 = -2 y . (c1 - c2) + ||c1||^2 - ||c2||^2 ,

so the only part of the prediction y that can change the pick is its projection onto the
candidate DIFFERENCE direction u = c1 - c2. In this corpus the candidates at a read are
observations of the same command shape whose file content differs (the trajectories are cat/mv/ls
over files whose bodies are canary hashes, csv blocks and log lines), so u lies in a
low-variance "which content is this" subspace, while the corpus spectrum is dominated by
empty-versus-nonempty output, format and length. Prediction error orthogonal to u is invisible to
the pick; error along u is the whole game.

THE MECHANISM AND HOW IT DIFFERS FROM THE PARENT

The parent keeps an exactly orthonormal Householder k-frame and rescales the target by exp(a_i)
along k selected directions. Its frame is steered by a log-volume term onto the LEADING
eigendirections of the target covariance, and its equalisation term drives the variance there
DOWN toward the untouched complement, so a_i is negative. I keep the frame machinery, the bounded scale
and the exact inverse, and change two things that together invert the flow:

1. The frame criterion becomes LOCAL discriminability instead of global variance. On each batch,
   every target's m=4 nearest neighbours are found in the fixed observation space (detached, exact
   squared distances), neighbour pairs closer than 0.002 of the mean pairwise squared distance are
   dropped as duplicates (the empty-output observations of mv and redirected cat render to
   literally identical text, so they sit at distance zero and would otherwise dilute the estimate),
   and the frame maximises mean_i [ log Var_local_i - 0.5 * log Var_global_i ], where Var_local is
   the variance of the surviving nearest-neighbour differences along frame direction i. This is
   local Fisher discriminant analysis / locality-preserving projection read as a determinant
   criterion: pick the k orthonormal directions along which observations that are ALREADY
   near-duplicates still differ. Those differences are exactly the same-shape-different-content
   differences that u is made of. The tempered denominator (eta=0.5) keeps globally dominant
   directions out; the criterion is bounded above because a nearest-neighbour difference variance
   is at most a constant times the global variance along the same direction, so there is no
   degenerate escape to zero global variance.

2. The scale is expansion-only: a_i = 2.0 * sigmoid(4 * spec_raw_i - 2) >= 0, so the map is
   Q diag(e^a, 1) Q^T with e^a >= 1 and its exact inverse Q diag(e^-a, 1) Q^T is a CONTRACTION in
   every direction. The equalisation term now pulls the post-transform variance along the selected
   directions UP to twice the mean variance of the untouched complement, rather than down to it.

WHY THIS SHOULD MOVE THE OBJECTIVE

Write the transform T and the model output p. Training compares p to T z; the eval compares
T^-1 p to the candidates. If the model were exact, T cancels: (T z) . (T^-1 u) = z . u, so the
decision variable is unchanged by construction and the transform cannot manufacture a score. What
it changes is the error term: with p = T z + eps, the decision variable carries eps . T^-1 u,
whose component along the selected directions is shrunk by e^-a. At the same time the loss sees
those directions scaled up by e^a, so the model is charged more for relative error there. The MSE
anchor charges e^{2a}; the ring-negative listwise term charges about e^a, because its detached
per-dimension precision weights w = (1/(mse+eps))^0.5 make the per-dimension contribution scale
like the per-dimension RMS error rather than its square. Neither cancels the expansion. So the
model buys accuracy on the content axes with capacity it was previously spending on format and
length axes that cannot affect any pick, and whatever error survives there is contracted before
the candidates are ranked. A second, smaller effect: the ring's confusability band-pass is computed
on target-target distances, which after expansion are content-weighted, so its "close but distinct"
band lands on content-distinct pairs instead of on format-distinct ones.

This is a bet about which subspace deserves the error budget, and it is neutral with respect to the
shortcut the metric does not cancel. comp_ca is a paired difference between the native chain and
the role-swapped chain over the same board; a predictor that keys on the location being read
contributes exactly zero in both arms whatever the target geometry is, because the transform is
applied identically in both. Sharpening the content axes can only pay if the model already carries
some signal about WHICH content the chain routed to that location — the prefix-content
cross-attention channel in the arch and the dual-address move-transport head are what would supply
it. The prediction is therefore that native_hit rises while swap_stayed does not, i.e. the
difference rises, and not that native accuracy rises uniformly.

WHAT I EXPECT TO SEE (all unmeasured — I cannot run the harness from the jail)

- comp_ca above the parent's -0.0262. I also expect it above the unmodified baseline's +0.0112,
  because I believe the parent's negative score comes from the sign of its flow: compressing the
  leading directions by up to e^-1.5 means its inverse AMPLIFIES prediction error there by up to
  4.5x in observation space, on axes the loss was simultaneously told to stop caring about, so its
  net observation-space error on those axes grows by up to e^{2*1.5} ~ 20x. My inverse is a
  contraction everywhere, so that failure mode is structurally unavailable.
- Learned scales settling near a ~ 0.6 to 1.0 (gain 1.8 to 2.7) rather than at the sigmoid ceiling:
  the equalisation goal is log-variance of the complement plus log 2, and the selected directions
  are low-variance but not vanishing.
- Health unchanged: the transform is bounded, orthonormal and exactly invertible, the regulariser
  is finite-guarded, and per-step cost over the parent is one 384x384 squared-distance matrix and
  a topk, which is negligible beside the unchanged 24-reflection Householder chain.

HOW IT COULD FAIL

- The strongest failure mode is that the mechanism is simply a no-op: if the model's relative error
  along a direction grows exactly in proportion to that direction's scale, the expansion and the
  inverse contraction cancel and nothing changes but the noise. The argument above says the
  proportionality is sub-linear, but it is an argument about a fixed point, not a measurement.
- The local-scatter criterion could select the encoder's high-frequency noise floor rather than
  content. The absolute (rather than purely ratio) form of the criterion is what guards this — pure
  noise directions have small absolute nearest-neighbour scatter — but the eta=0.5 tempering moves
  part-way toward the ratio and therefore part-way toward that failure.
- If the model carries no content signal at all at the read position, expanding the content axes
  amplifies whatever bias it does have, which is as likely to hurt as to help; the paired difference
  makes that neutral in expectation but the scored slice is one frozen realization.
