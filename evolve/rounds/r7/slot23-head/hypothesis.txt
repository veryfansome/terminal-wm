WHAT THE PARENT DOES AND WHY I THINK IT STALLS

The parent addresses an abstract 64-dimensional slot memory: from each command embedding it makes a
unit source key and a unit destination key, reads the slot at the source key, writes a move-gated blend
into the destination slot, erases the source. Chaining works only if the destination key of "mv X Y"
lands on the same slot direction as the source key of "mv Y Z". Two things make that hard here, and both
are visible in the data. First, every command in a cups window is a near-identical string: the windows in
evolve/jail_data/train_sample.jsonl move files whose names differ by a single trailing character
(.../sshd_config.cfg.6 -> .../sshd_config.cfg.7 sitting next to .../sshd_config.cfg.2 ->
.../sshd_config.cfg.4). A frozen mean-pooled sentence embedding of such a window is dominated by a large
component that every command shares; the discriminative part is a small residual. Keys built by a fixed
linear map of that raw embedding, then unit-normalised, are all nearly parallel, so a soft-addressed
fast-weight memory reads a blur of every slot rather than one slot. Second, no fixed linear layer can
remove that shared component, because the component is a property of the WINDOW, not of the corpus: it
is a window-dependent mean, and a Linear can only subtract a constant.

WHAT I CHANGE

(1) Causal window standardisation of the command embeddings before any key is formed. At step j the
module subtracts a running mean over the window's own commands up to and including j, and divides by a
running per-window variance, both shrunk toward a LEARNED global mean vector and a learned global scale
by a fixed prior count (4). This is the shrinkage estimator of the window statistics: at step 0 it falls
back to the learned global mean (initialised at zero, which is exactly the corpus mean of the
standardised embeddings, so the module starts at "raw embedding" and the centering strengthens as
evidence accumulates), and by the scored read position — always the LAST command of the window — the
estimate has seen every command in the window, so the read query is the one that gets the full benefit.
It is strictly causal: only commands at or before j, never any observation, enter the estimate.
This is the "all-but-the-top" / common-component-removal result from the sentence-embedding literature
(Mu & Viswanath), imported as an equation rather than a metaphor and made causal and learnable.

(2) The abstract slot memory is replaced by an assignment over the window's own earlier commands. Score
s(j,i) = <W_src c_j, W_dst c_i>/tau - rho*(j-i-1) on the standardised embeddings, masked strictly causal.
W_src and W_dst are a symmetry-broken pair (dst initialised as src plus small noise), which is the one
thing I keep from the parent's dual extractor.

(3) The scores become links by log-domain Sinkhorn normalisation with a learned dustbin row and column
(SuperGlue's matching layer, five iterations, marginals 1 per real row and column and n on each
dustbin). This is the mechanism I am actually betting on. The ground truth in these windows is a partial
MATCHING, not an independent choice per row: in the sample window above the true links are 9<-8, 11<-9,
12<-10, 13<-12, 14<-13, 15<-14, 16<-15, 10<-6, 8<-7 — every earlier step is consumed by at most one
later step. Independent row softmaxes (what the parent, and every attention-style resolver, does) cannot
express that constraint; Sinkhorn's column marginal does, and it is exactly the discriminator the
near-identical filenames need: when step 13 scores nearly as well against step 11 as against step 12, the
fact that step 11 is already claimed by step 14 pushes 13 onto 12. Column exclusivity therefore replaces
the explicit erasure term such a resolver would otherwise need.

(4) The chain is resolved to unbounded depth in closed form. A learned relay gate m_i, fed by the step's
command features and its paired observation, says whether a step delivers its own observation (an
exposing read) or relays what it received (a move, whose observation is empty and identical across every
move in the window). content(j) = sum_i a(j,i) [ m_i content(i) + (1-m_i) o_i ] is strictly lower
triangular in content, so I - A diag(m) is unit lower triangular and one triangular solve gives the exact
fixed point. No sequential scan, no hop cap, and the query is built from the command alone so the
trailing command of an odd-length window — whose observation IS the answer and is absent — still issues a
full query and gets a full resolution. No content ever depends on its own step's observation: the
assignment is strictly lower triangular, so the injected prediction at cmd_r uses only obs_<r.

(5) Injection and aux. The resolved content enters the arch's own prediction at command positions under
a sigmoid gate, through a zero-init (D,D) readout plus a small non-zero per-dimension direct scale
(0.02), so the wrapped net is a 0.005-scale perturbation of the unwrapped net at initialisation while
every resolver parameter still receives gradient from the main loss AT the scored read position. The
train-only aux is label-free from the batch alone: a relay-gate calibration against steps whose
observation sits in an oversized duplicate cluster (the empty-output moves), a commitment term that
pushes each relay step's assignment row onto a single predecessor (weight 0.05, relay weights detached
so the gate cannot dodge it by collapsing), and, on mined reads whose observation duplicates an earlier
one across at least one content-free step, a listwise ranking of both the resolved content and the
head-augmented prediction against the contentful observations seen so far, under the eval's own
per-dimension squared-L2 decision variable.

WHY THIS SHOULD MOVE THE OBJECTIVE

comp_ca is the paired difference between the native chain and a chain in which two contents exchange
their move-position sets over the same board, same destinations, same depth. Everything that keys on
which location is being read is identical in both arms and cancels per window; the only thing that
survives is carrying a particular content's identity through the links. This head's prediction at the
read position is a convex combination of the window's own earlier observations, selected by which
earlier commands it links to — and role-swapping the chain rewrites exactly those move commands, so a
resolver that links correctly returns a different observation in the two arms and a resolver that does
not returns the same blend and contributes zero, not a negative. The recency term is the one part that
could read as chain-position keying rather than content keying, so it is initialised small (0.05) and
capped, with the content match as the dominant term; if measurement reports shortcut leaning, that
parameter is where to look.

WHAT I EXPECT TO SEE, ALL UNMEASURED (I cannot run the harness from the jail)

Beating the parent's +0.0037 needs the links to be better than blur, not perfect. The falsifiable claims,
in order of how much they would tell us: (a) native pick rate rises above the parent's while the swapped
"held" rate does not rise with it — that is the signature of real carrying rather than a stronger prior;
(b) the deeper slice (d4+, n=29) improves at least as much as d3, because the closed-form solve makes
depth free whereas a fixed-hop or blurred scan degrades with depth; (c) if the Sinkhorn column constraint
is what matters, the gain should be concentrated in windows with interleaved chains, where two contents
are in flight at once and independent row softmaxes have no way to keep them apart. The way this fails
is that the residual after window centering is still too small for the frozen encoder to have kept the
one-character filename distinction at all, in which case the assignment is near-uniform over the recent
past, content is a blur, the gate learns to close, and the score sits at the parent's level rather than
below it. Wall clock should not be a problem and may improve: the parent's per-step sequential scan over
command positions is replaced by one batched triangular solve plus five 33x33 Sinkhorn iterations.
