AXES CHANGED: head only (r2_dualaddress_move_transport -> r7_move_ablation_counterfactual_contrast).
Every other axis keeps the parent's impl and params verbatim. Nothing else needs to change: the new
head registers no parameters and does not touch net.forward, so the arch, optim, stream, batcher,
objective and target all see exactly the model they saw in the parent run.

THE CROSSOVER. The partner is the all-baseline genome, whose head is baseline_passthrough: the
arch's own readout, unchanged, and a forward that is bit-for-bit the unwrapped forward. My parent's
head is the opposite: it bolts a second content-routing mechanism (a dual-address delta-rule
transport memory) onto a model whose ARCH is already a content-routing mechanism (the provenance
chase), and injects that memory's read into the prediction through a learned scale. I take the
partner's contribution literally -- the prediction path goes back to being the arch's alone, no
injected read, no parallel memory, no re-pointed forward -- and I keep the parent's contribution
where it is actually load-bearing: its label-free mining of "this observation is the same content
that appeared earlier under a different command, with content-free steps in between", and its
squared-L2 InfoNCE over the row's own observations. Then I spend the head's whole budget on
something neither parent had: an INTERVENTION.

THE MECHANISM. At train time only, the head runs the net a second time on two edited copies of the
mined rows and scores the paired difference between them. It never edits the eval path.

  1. Mining (label-free, from the batch tensors alone, no_grad). Content-free steps are found as the
     one large duplicate cluster every observation batch carries: a move or a redirect prints
     nothing, so all such steps render to the same observation text and collapse to one embedding.
     A read step j is mined when its observation duplicates an earlier contentful observation i
     under a command that is not a duplicate, no earlier step has both the same command and the same
     observation, and at least one content-free step sits between i and j.
  2. BROKEN arm: mask out (key_pad + zeroed token, both the command and its observation) every
     content-free step strictly between the content's first exposure and the read. That removes the
     only mechanism that could have put that content at that location.
  3. CONTROL arm: mask out the SAME NUMBER of steps, drawn from steps that provably cannot change
     what sits there -- earlier contentful reads that are not that content (a read mutates nothing),
     and steps after the read (a causal model cannot see them).
  4. Loss = pos_weight * (-log p_control(C)) + diff_weight * softplus(log p_broken(C)
     - log p_control(C) + margin), where p(C) is the mass the prediction at the read position puts
     on the mined content under one shared candidate set -- the row's contentful observations up to
     j, scored by squared L2 at temperature cand_temp.

WHY IT SHOULD MOVE THE OBJECTIVE. comp_ca is a paired difference over two arms that share the board,
the depth, the destinations and the read, and differ only in which content each move carries. A
predictor that keys on WHICH LOCATION is asked about cancels to exactly zero there. This aux is the
same shape of question asked of the training data: both arms carry the same read command at the same
position, the same observation tokens, and the same number of masked steps, so a location-keyed
predictor scores the identical p(C) in both arms and eats the full softplus(margin) penalty; so does
a predictor whose only sensitivity is "how much of the stream is masked". The only way to make the
term small is to be sensitive to WHICH moves happened between the exposure and the read -- which is
the capability the metric is trying to detect. The parent's aux could be satisfied by naming the
content that "belongs" to a location; this one cannot.

Second reason: with no parameters of its own, every gradient this aux produces lands on the arch's
provenance chase -- the role-unbinding diagonals, the recency and null terms, the stop statistic,
the content readout. The chase is currently trained only end-to-end, through six hops of soft
mixture, by a loss averaged over all positions, most of which are ordinary reads that need no chase
at all. This puts a direct, hard signal on the handful of positions per row where the chase is the
only thing that can produce the answer.

WHAT I VERIFIED, AND WHAT I DID NOT. Against evolve/jail_data/train_sample.jsonl (24 real
trajectories) I checked the mining and the intervention with a filesystem simulator that reproduces
all 365 real read outputs exactly: on every one of the 24 selected examples the broken arm's true
answer at the read is no longer the mined content, and the control arm's true answer still is, with
matched mask counts and the read itself never masked. Before adding the min_first / max_span /
max_ablate gates, 6 of 24 selected examples were coincidentally identical directory listings (`ls -1
/` vs a later bare `ls`) for which the moves are irrelevant and the contrast would push the wrong
way; the gates remove all of those. I also checked: the wrapped net has zero new parameters, zero
new attributes and an unchanged forward; the odd-length scored layout [cmd_0, obs_0, ..., cmd_r]
runs through the arch untouched because the head is inert outside training; the aux returns a hard
0.0 on short, malformed or unmined batches; every arch parameter receives gradient from the combined
loss; and on a 64-row batch through the real r4 stream collate a full step costs about 1.35x the
no-aux step on CPU, with the aux falling from ~2.0 to ~0.17 over 30 overfit steps. I have NOT
measured comp_ca or any fitness -- no performance claim here is measured, and I could not run the
real harness from the jail.

BIGGEST RISK. The parent's +0.6142 may rest on the dual-address transport memory I am deleting, in
which case removing a working parallel router costs more than supervising the arch's router gains,
and this lands below the parent. A second risk: the model can make the broken arm wrong by detecting
mid-sequence padding rather than by tracking content, and although the matched-count control arm is
designed to price that shortcut out, the two arms still differ in WHERE the holes are, so some of
that route may remain open.
