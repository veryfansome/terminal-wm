CROSSOVER: parent r6-01-slotconflict-deficit-scheduler x the dual-address partner.
Axes changed: target (new impl), stream (partner's impl adopted), head (one param retuned to
keep the partner's stream from silently disabling the parent's aux loss). arch, objective,
optim and batcher are the parent's, verbatim.

WHAT THE READ ACTUALLY DECIDES

The scored decision is argmin over candidate contents of the squared L2 distance between the
read-position prediction and each candidate's observation embedding. In this data the candidates
at a window's read are whole file contents, and a content renders to the SAME observation text
wherever it currently sits (cwd is constant, exit is 0, the payload is the file body) - verified
against evolve/jail_data/train_sample.jsonl, where a file read before a move and after a move
produces byte-identical output. So prediction error only ever flips a decision through its
projection onto the DIFFERENCES between co-present contents. Error along any direction in which
the candidates agree is free; error along the directions in which mutually confusable contents
differ is the entire decision. Plain MSE in the observation space (the parent's identity target)
weights all 768 directions equally and therefore spends most of its gradient on the high-variance
directions that separate a cat from an ls from a uname - directions on which every candidate at a
cups read already agrees.

THE MECHANISM (axis target, new impl fisher_volume_metric_flow)

Train in a linearly remapped observation space and read back through the exact algebraic inverse,
where the map is LEARNED BY GRADIENT from the training loss and constrained to unit determinant:

    W = diag(exp(g)) . (I + N_low) . (I + N_up)
    g = cap * tanh(s/cap) then mean-centred  ->  det diag(exp(g)) = exp(sum g) = 1 exactly
    N_low strictly lower, N_up strictly upper, each Frobenius-renormalised to <= 0.35 in the
    forward  ->  both factors unit-triangular, det 1, always invertible, spectrum in [0.65,1.35]

make_target(z_obs, z_prev) = W z_obs. to_obs(pred, z_prev) = W^-1 pred, assembled from the same
three factors by a reciprocal gain and two unit-triangular solves. Determinant one is the whole
safety argument: a learned target that can shrink collapses, and a learned target that can inflate
makes an in-batch contrastive trivially easy. Volume preservation forbids both, so the map can only
trade one direction against another - it is forced to be a choice of METRIC, never a change of
scale. Condition number is bounded by construction (<= ~18 with these caps), so the inverse cannot
amplify prediction error without limit. All parameters are zero-initialised, so at step 1 the map
is exactly the identity and the genome is exactly the parent; every departure has to be paid for by
the loss. This is what makes it a strict, bounded extension of the parent rather than a gamble.

Two opposed pressures shape W, and their equilibrium is the point.

(1) The main loss (the parent's ring-negative listwise contrastive plus its anchor MSE) pulls W
toward shrinking the directions where the model's residual is large. Under a fixed determinant,
minimising E||W e||^2 has the solution W proportional to Sigma_e^-1/2: a whitening of the model's
own residual covariance. On its own that is the wrong half - it would happily amplify whatever the
model already predicts easily, which is exactly the shared cat-format directions, and then the
inverse would amplify the residual on the content directions at read time.

(2) reg() supplies the missing Fisher numerator. Inside make_target the module caches a band-pass
CONFUSABILITY ring over the batch's target-target squared distances - the same band-pass shape the
parent's objective already trusts: near-identical targets (the repeated-file case, which is common
here because contents recur verbatim) get about zero weight, close-but-distinct targets take the
mass. reg() returns

    -beta * log( sum_ij ring_ij * ||W z_i - W z_j||^2 / D ),   ring detached, beta = 0.05

i.e. minus the log of the ring-weighted mean squared separation of confusable targets MEASURED IN
THE MAPPED SPACE. Minimising it means maximising that separation. Because W is linear, W z_i - W z_j
is just the difference of the targets make_target already computed, so the whole term costs one
192x192 gram matrix per step and nothing else. Gradient reaches only W; z is detached.

Together, under det(W) = 1: stretch the directions along which co-present, mutually confusable
contents actually differ, shrink the directions the model cannot resolve anyway. That is an online
Fisher discriminant on the target space, learned jointly with the model instead of estimated
offline, and the unit-determinant constraint is what makes the ratio well-posed. The translated
outside-the-domain mechanism is the volume-preserving flow layer from normalising flows (a
det-1 diagonal-plus-triangular factorisation with an exact triangular-solve inverse) used not to
model a density but to carry a learned metric.

WHY IT SHOULD MOVE comp_ca SPECIFICALLY

comp_ca is native_hit minus swap_stayed on the same board. Both arms are decided by the same
argmin over the same candidate set; what differs is which content the chain routed to the read.
Raising the resolution of the model in exactly the directions that separate co-present contents
raises native_hit and, symmetrically, makes the model less likely to return the native content
under the swapped chain, because the two contents become further apart in the metric the loss is
actually optimising. A transform of the target space cannot manufacture the location-keying
cancellation and cannot manufacture a first-mover lookup: it does not see commands, positions or
order at all, only the answer embedding. It changes only how much a given residual costs. So
whatever it buys is bought on the content axis.

WHY THE STREAM CHANGES WITH IT (axis stream -> r4_pathslot_dualaddress_code)

This is the crossover half. The partner's mechanism overwrites three hash-chosen coordinate slots
of every command embedding with a purely lexical dual-address code: a 96-bit Rademacher hash of the
command's source path, the same family on its destination path, and a 16-dim structural tag. The
same path string yields the identical vector wherever it appears, so a move's DESTINATION slot and
a later read's SOURCE slot hold the identical 96-dim vector exactly when the two paths are equal,
and near-orthogonal vectors otherwise. The parent's arch is r22, whose delta-rule file memory and
path memory write a raw observation at a key linear in the command embedding and read it back at a
query linear in the command embedding. Those read/write projections currently have to invent path
identity out of a frozen sentence embedding of the whole command line; with the dual-address code
they can align source and destination addresses by an exact vector match. That is the single most
direct enabler of "which content is now at this location", and it is why the partner's stream, not
its arch, is the piece worth importing: it composes with the parent's memory instead of replacing
it. The code is a pure per-command function exposed as code_cmds(cmds, z_cmd) with no cross-command
state, so the instrument can reproduce the tokens on the native chain and on the role-swapped chain
independently. The two changed axes are orthogonal by construction: the stream reshapes the COMMAND
space, the target reshapes the OBSERVATION space, and neither reads the other's tensors.

WHY THE HEAD PARAM MOVES (axis head, path_thresh 0.60 -> 0.45)

The parent's head mines (pre-read, mutating command, future read) triples by thresholding cosine
similarity between raw command rows of the batch, and those rows are now the coded ones. The code
replaces 208 of 768 coordinates with +-1 values whose squared norm is 208, which is what
standardised coordinates already carried, so the cosine DENOMINATOR is essentially unchanged while
the numerator loses the contribution those 208 coordinates used to make: every cosine is scaled by
roughly 560/768 = 0.73, plus 96/768 = 0.125 when two commands share a source path. The triples the
head most wants - a read of the move's DESTINATION path paired with the move itself - match on
different slots (read source vs move destination) and so get the 0.73 dilution with no bonus. At the
inherited 0.60 threshold those triples stop being mined and the aux loss silently goes to zero,
which is a regression against the parent for a reason that has nothing to do with the head. 0.60 x
0.73 = 0.44, so 0.45 approximately preserves the parent's mining behaviour under the new stream.
This is arithmetic on the coding scheme, not a tuned number, and it is unmeasured.

FALSIFIABLE PREDICTIONS

- comp_ca rises above the parent's +0.0337. If the learned metric is doing what is claimed, the
  gain shows up as a higher native_hit at unchanged-or-lower swap_stayed, not as both arms rising.
- The map does not stay at the identity: because reg() always pushes for expansion along confusable
  directions while the main loss resists, the Frobenius caps should bind early and W should sit at
  a bounded, non-trivial tilt for the rest of training.
- FALSIFIED IF: comp_ca lands at or below the parent while the health/reconstruction readings stay
  normal. That would mean the residual-whitening pressure (1) dominates the discriminant pressure
  (2) - the map is amplifying the easy shared-format directions and the inverse is paying for it on
  the content directions at read time - and the fix is a larger beta, not a different family.
- FALSIFIED ALSO IF: the reconstruction/health reading degrades. With det(W) = 1 and a bounded
  condition number that should be impossible for the target to cause, so a degraded reading would
  point at the stream swap instead, and the two changes would need separating.

PERFORMANCE (all unmeasured, no miniature run was used)

Two extra 768x768 parameters plus a 768-vector, about 1.18M parameters, ~4.7MB, trained by the same
AdamW group as the rest. Per training step the target costs two 768x768 matmuls against the batch's
command rows plus one 192x192 gram - small against the trunk. The optimiser's spectral cap does
touch both (768,768) parameters, but the forward renormalises them to a fixed Frobenius norm, so any
uniform rescale the cap applies is exactly absorbed and the effective map is unchanged. to_obs costs
two unit-triangular solves and one fused 768x768 matmul per call, on whatever device the caller's
tensor lives on (device and dtype are taken from the argument, so a CPU read-back works). Neither
make_target nor to_obs touches z_prev, indexes a position, or assumes anything about sequence
length or parity, so the odd-length scored layout [cmd_0, obs_0, ..., cmd_r] is handled identically
to any other input: it is a per-row map. The forward/inverse algebra and det(W) = 1 were verified
numerically outside the harness on a small dimension (round-trip error at machine epsilon).
