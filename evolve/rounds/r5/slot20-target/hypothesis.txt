CANDIDATE: confusable-contrast metric target, on a crossover trunk.

WHAT THE CROSSOVER KEEPS FROM EACH PROGRAM

From the parent I keep the two ROUTING mechanisms it already carries: the arch
r24_backchain_pointer_resolution (the K-hop backward pointer chase, which is the only mechanism in
either genome that composes several address matches into one retrieval, i.e. that can span a chain
of depth > 1) and the head r2_dualaddress_move_transport (a dual-address delta-rule transport
memory injected into the prediction, which is the same transport mechanism the partner's arch
r23_dual_address_transport_pointer embodies, so keeping it keeps the partner's memory idea too).

From the partner I keep its LOSS, r4_relocation_address_ring_quotient, in place of the parent's
r12_antiretrieval_ring_negatives. r12 is a batch-wide contrast over confusable targets. r4 is r12
plus command-orthogonalization plus a second head that is exactly the shape of the scored decision:
it discovers, label-free and from the batch alone, RELOCATION ROWS -- a row whose target is an exact
duplicate of an earlier target in the SAME trajectory under a DIFFERENT command, with the large
duplicate class (the silent no-output observation every mv and every redirect shares) excluded --
and forces a within-trajectory choice on those rows against candidates ranked by an address ring
over command-embedding similarity. In this data a relocation row is literally "cat <path> after the
content that now lives at <path> was moved there", which is the event comp_ca reads. Nothing in the
parent's loss singles those rows out. r4 needs WANTS_CTX plumbing, which baseline_interleave
supplies via extract_cmd_input, and it reads trajectory boundaries off the strict-causal previous
observation, which is untouched by anything below.

WHAT I ADDED, AND WHAT IT COMPUTES THAT NEITHER PROGRAM COMPUTES

Both programs train with target = identity: the loss charges prediction error uniformly across all
768 observation dimensions. My target impl replaces that with a global symmetric linear map and its
exact inverse. The map is built from a quantity neither program forms anywhere: C, the covariance of
UNIT DIFFERENCE DIRECTIONS between mutually confusable observations.

Estimation, every accum_every steps, from the batch of next-observation targets only: subsample up
to sub_rows of them, form all pairwise squared distances, drop each row's near-duplicate partners by
a per-row relative floor (dup_frac x that row's mean distance), take the k nearest surviving
partners per row, then keep only the closest keep_q fraction of those collected pairs BY RANK. Each
kept pair contributes (z_i - z_j)/||z_i - z_j||; C is the running EMA of the mean outer product of
those unit differences, so trace(C) = 1 by construction. The two prunes matter and are not
decoration. The duplicate floor removes the silent-observation cluster's internal pairs and the
same-content-read-twice pairs, whose difference is ~0 and whose normalized direction would be pure
noise. The rank prune removes the pairs that survive the floor only because a row has no close
non-duplicate neighbour at all -- for a silent row every non-duplicate neighbour is a contentful
observation, and admitting those would load C with the huge silent-vs-content direction, which is
shared by every candidate in a window and therefore contributes exactly nothing to any forced
choice.

C is then rescaled to unit mean eigenvalue, shrunk toward the identity, eigendecomposed in float64
on CPU, and split into fwd = V diag(lam^(p/2)) V^T and inv = V diag(lam^(-p/2)) V^T, with one common
scalar chosen so that the mean of lam^p is 1 (the transformed target keeps unit average
per-dimension second moment). fwd and inv are mutually exact inverses by eigenvalue construction,
not by an iterative approximation. make_target left-multiplies the target by fwd; to_obs
left-multiplies the prediction by inv and hands back the fixed observation space the retrieval eval
uses.

WHY THIS SHOULD CHANGE THE MEASURED QUANTITY

The retrieval decision is not "how close is the prediction to the truth". For a prediction p, a true
content z_t and a foil z_f, the eval's decision variable difference is
||p - z_t||^2 - ||p - z_f||^2 = -2 p.(z_t - z_f) + ||z_t||^2 - ||z_f||^2, so with e = p - z_t and
delta = z_t - z_f the choice flips exactly when e.delta > ||delta||^2 / 2. Every component of the
prediction error orthogonal to delta cancels identically and cannot cost a single window. Under
target = identity the loss charges ||e||^2, which spends the model's capacity in proportion to the
total variance of the observation distribution -- dominated by silent-vs-contentful, by image and by
file-format directions, all of which are shared across the candidates a window forces a choice
between and all of which therefore cancel. Training in the fwd metric charges e^T C^p e, and at p = 1
that is exactly E over confusable pairs of (e.delta_hat)^2, the mean squared perturbation of the very
statistic that flips the forced choice. p interpolates: p = 0 is the untouched loss, p = 1 is the
decision-variable metric, and I run p = 0.7 with shrinkage 0.1 because I cannot tune here and want
the map's condition number bounded (with those settings it is single-digit). The amplification
inv applies to the model's output in low-contrast directions is harmless for the measurement by the
same cancellation identity above -- it is common to true and foil.

This is the discriminative / matched-filter weighting move from detection theory, and the
neighbourhood-difference estimator is the chunklet-difference construction of Relevant Component
Analysis run with the sign reversed: RCA whitens by a difference covariance to suppress it, here the
difference covariance is between the things that must be told apart, so it is COLORED rather than
whitened. That sign is the whole design, and it is the opposite of the archived
zca_shrunk_target_whitening, which colors by the inverse of the GLOBAL second moment -- a matrix
whose leading directions are precisely the ones that cancel in this measurement.

Mechanistically the effect is a re-allocation of loss pressure, not a new input. It should raise
native_hit only through the model becoming better at saying WHICH content the routing landed on --
which is what comp_ca isolates, since a predictor keyed on the location being read is identical in
both arms and differences to exactly zero however sharp its content geometry is.

WHERE THE MECHANISM SITS RELATIVE TO THE SCORED POSITION

Nothing here is gated on an observation being present. make_target runs at train time only (it is
additionally guarded on self.training and torch.is_grad_enabled()). to_obs is a single unconditional
matrix multiply of the prediction; it ignores z_prev entirely, so it cannot switch off at the scored
command whose own observation is withheld. No resolved routing enters the model's input: the model
sees exactly the token stream it saw before, and the mv chain is still something it must resolve
from the command embeddings alone. The map is one global matrix, identical for every step, every
window, every candidate and both arms of the role swap, so it cannot express any per-window
information and cannot move a paired difference by favouring one arm.

The module registers buffers only, no parameters, so the optim chunk's Muon key-shape router and its
(D,D) spectral cap see nothing new, and reg() is 0.0.

WHAT WOULD HAVE TO BE TRUE FOR THIS TO FAIL

1. If the bottleneck is routing rather than readout -- if the backchain chase and the transport
   memory are not actually identifying which content ended up at the read location -- then sharpening
   the metric of the readout changes nothing and the number does not move.
2. If the frozen encoder places the difference between two confusable cups contents in directions
   that are dominated by incidental variation (line count, punctuation, file length) rather than the
   canary token, then C is loaded with nuisance and coloring by it amplifies the wrong axes. My
   defence is that the foils at score time ARE other confusable observations, so the directions that
   separate them are by definition the directions the decision uses; but if the encoder makes those
   directions unlearnable, the extra pressure is wasted and could hurt.
3. If the step budget is short enough that warmup_accum x accum_every steps (192 steps by default)
   is a large fraction of training, the map is the identity for most of the run and the candidate
   degenerates to the crossover with no target effect.
4. If the objective swap from r12 to r4 is itself a regression on this trunk, the target mechanism
   has to carry both changes. That is the deliberate risk of a crossover rather than a point
   mutation; r4's relocation head is the single most on-objective mechanism in either program and is
   worth the coupling.
