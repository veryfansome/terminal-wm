CANDIDATE: r5_depth_stratified_epoch_reshuffle_blocks (axis: batcher; no other axis changed)

WHAT THE TWO PROGRAMS I WAS GIVEN DO, AND WHAT I KEPT FROM EACH

Parent (r2_routed_collision_depth_batcher) replays every training sequence's command list
through a symbolic content tracker that understands mv, cp, `cat A > B` and `cat A >> B`, so it
recovers a hop count for every read whose content was routed there. It groups sequences into
collision buckets keyed by (image, digit-normalized read path, hop count) and draws distinct
members from one bucket. Its selection pressure toward depth is a soft multinomial tilt:
bucket weight = len(members)**0.5 * exp(0.8 * depth), and only up to 50% of a batch is drawn
that way at all.

The crossover partner (r2_routedepth_tilt_readcollision_blocks) walks the mv edges backwards
from each `cat P` to get the exact hop count of the DEEPEST routed read in a sequence, and then
uses the embeddings themselves: under a fixed Johnson-Lindenstrauss projection it forms
neighbourhoods of sequences whose read-command embeddings are near-duplicates while their
read-ANSWER embeddings are not, and applies an annealed Boltzmann tilt exp(alpha*z) on the
z-scored depth.

I kept from the parent: the symbolic tracker, which is strictly richer than the partner's
mv-only 3-token walk (it also follows the cvar-style `cat s0.dat > out.dat` and
`cat s0.dat >> acc.dat` chains that make up a large share of this data), and the
(image, normalized path shape, hop count) collision key.
I kept from the partner: the "same query, different answer" test in embedding space, and the
easy-to-hard annealing of the depth pressure. The embedding test is now applied INSIDE each
symbolic bucket rather than as a global k-NN, so a block is a set of sequences that the
symbolic key says share a read shape AND the projection says agree on the read command while
disagreeing on the read answer.

WHAT THIS CANDIDATE COMPUTES THAT NEITHER PARENT COMPUTES

Both parents express their preference for deep chains as a WEIGHT in a with-replacement draw.
Neither of them controls the realized composition of a batch by depth, and in both of them the
depth preference is fought by a size term: in the parent, bucket weight carries len**0.5, so a
200-member depth-1 bucket (weight 14.1*e^0.8 = 31.4) still outweighs a 3-member depth-3 bucket
(weight 1.73*e^2.4 = 19.1); in the partner the tilt is on a z-score that is re-centred by the
whole population, so a rare tail is pulled back toward the mean. On top of that both leave
25%-50% of every batch as plain uniform draws.

This candidate replaces the tilt with an EXACT INTEGER QUOTA per hop-count stratum, and
replaces with-replacement drawing with random reshuffling inside each stratum.

  1. Every sequence gets one label: d = min(hop count of its deepest routed read, 4). This
     partitions the training set into disjoint strata S_0..S_4 (not overlapping buckets).
  2. A batch is n_base = round(0.25*bs) uniform slots plus n_str = bs - n_base stratified slots.
     The stratified slots are allocated by largest-remainder rounding of
        p_d proportional to |S_d| * exp(depth_beta * d * r),   r = min(1, step/(ramp_frac*T))
     so the allocation is EXACTLY the empirical stratum distribution at step 1 and reaches the
     tilted distribution after the ramp. Largest-remainder rounding means the realized count per
     stratum in each batch is the target count, not a Binomial around it.
  3. Each stratum quota is clamped by caps[d] = min(|S_d|, bs, max_repeat*bs*|S_d|/n), which
     bounds how many times a sequence in that stratum is visited relative to the rate uniform
     sampling would give it, and strata with fewer than min_stratum=16 members are excluded from
     the quota entirely (they remain reachable through the uniform base). Surplus from a clamped
     stratum is redistributed to the next deepest stratum with headroom, and any residue falls
     through to the uniform fill.
  4. Stratum slots are filled from a per-stratum shuffled epoch cursor (torch.randperm, refilled
     on exhaustion). Inside a stratum the draw is therefore WITHOUT replacement until the whole
     stratum has been seen. Both parents draw with replacement everywhere (torch.randint,
     torch.multinomial(replacement=True)), which under a fixed step budget both wastes slots on
     repeats and leaves a long tail of rare deep sequences unvisited for stretches. Random
     reshuffling is the standard variance-reduced alternative to i.i.d. SGD sampling.
  5. With probability block_frac_max*r a run of a stratum's slots is filled from a collision
     block of that same stratum instead of from the cursor, giving the in-batch hard-negative
     geometry both parents were after; block members are picked without replacement, tilted by
     1 + (number of routed reads in that sequence).

Concretely, on a plausible shape of the training set (n = 6000, |S_0..S_4| =
1800/2400/1200/480/120, bs = 64), this candidate allocates the 48 stratified slots as roughly
2/7/12/15/12, so 42% of every batch after the ramp carries a depth-3-or-deeper chain and the
depth-4 sequences are visited about 9x more often per sequence than uniform sampling would
visit them; the caps hold that below the 20x ceiling. Neither parent produces anything close to
that reallocation, because in both of them the size term and the uniform remainder cap the
achievable emphasis.

WHY THIS SHOULD MOVE THE MEASURED QUANTITY

comp_ca is measured only on windows of depth 3 and depth 4+ (n=89, d3 60, d4+ 29), on images
the model never trained on. The skill being measured — carrying a content's identity across a
chain of moves — is a function of chain length, and a model trained mostly on 0- and 1-hop
material has almost no gradient signal for composing 3 and 4 hops. If deep chains are a small
minority of the training split (which the depth structure of this data suggests: the cups
windows in the sample are depth 1), then the single largest thing a batcher can do is buy the
deep stratum an order of magnitude more gradient steps without letting a handful of sequences be
memorized. That is exactly what the quota + cap + reshuffle does, and it is a change in kind
rather than degree relative to a multinomial tilt fighting a size term.

The second effect is on the negative pool. Because a whole sequence's steps all enter the
batch, a stratified batch after the ramp puts many depth-3/4 routed reads in the same step; the
collision blocks additionally guarantee that several of them share a normalized read path while
their answers are verified distinct in projection. A predictor that answers by path name or by
lexical command match cannot separate those, so whatever contrastive or equivalence pressure the
objective applies is applied where it discriminates content rather than location.

WHY THIS IS NOT LEAKAGE

Everything this module computes is computed once at setup, from the train split's command
strings and the train split's own frozen embeddings, and its only output is a list of integer
INDICES. Nothing resolved by the tracker is ever written into a model input, a target, or a
scoring-time tensor; the model still has to learn routing from tokens. The batcher is not called
at scoring time at all, so the withheld observation at the scored position cannot switch any
part of this mechanism off: the depth labels, the strata and the blocks come only from training
sequences, where every observation is present. In particular nothing here is gated on an
observation existing at the position being judged.

WHAT WOULD HAVE TO BE TRUE FOR THIS TO FAIL

- If the training split has essentially no depth-3+ sequences, the min_stratum=16 floor zeroes
  those quotas and the mechanism degrades to a mild tilt over depth 1-2, which is close to the
  parent. Result: no gain, no loss.
- If the training split is already depth-rich, the reallocation is small and the whole thing is
  within noise of the parent.
- If over-exposing the deep stratum costs enough general next-observation fitting that the
  native arm drops as much as the swapped arm, the paired difference does not move. The
  base_frac=0.25 uniform reserve and the max_repeat=20 cap are the two knobs that bound this,
  and they are the first things to relax or tighten on a re-run.
- If the objective r22_exact_target_equivalence_quotient makes no use of in-batch negatives, the
  collision-block half of the design is inert; the stratification half is objective-agnostic and
  still applies. I did not adopt the partner's objective/arch/head, because I cannot read their
  source from this jail and swapping them blind would confound the batcher measurement rather
  than combine mechanisms; the crossover is done where I can see both programs, on my own axis.
- The symbolic tracker inherits the parent's approximations: it ignores pipes and multi-command
  lines, treats `mv` as removing the source's content, and caps depth at 4. A systematically
  wrong depth label would stratify on the wrong variable and the quota would amplify the error
  instead of the signal.

UNMEASURED. I could not run the harness from this jail, so no number here is measured. The setup
cost is one pass of command parsing over the train split (the same cost the parent already pays)
plus at most max_filter_buckets=1200 small projections of at most bucket_cap=32 rows each; the
per-step cost is a handful of small multinomial/randperm calls, comparable to the parent's.
