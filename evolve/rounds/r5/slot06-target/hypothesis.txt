CANDIDATE: neighbor_contrast_shaped_target (axis: target). Only the target axis is changed; the
rest of the genome is the parent's, unchanged.

WHAT THE CROSSOVER KEEPS FROM EACH PROGRAM
The two programs differ on the target axis exactly as identity vs ridge_zca_whitened_obs. The map
I propose contains both as parameter settings of one family: the forward map is

    F  =  Cov_contrast^(q/2) . Cov_total^(-a/2)          (defaults q = 1.0, a = 0.5)
    F^-1 = Cov_total^(a/2) . Cov_contrast^(-q/2)

so q = a = 0 is the parent's identity, and q = 0, a > 0 is a ridge-shrunk symmetric whitening of
the target space -- the partner's mechanism. The new term is Cov_contrast, which neither program
computes. From the rest of the partner's genome I deliberately keep nothing, and the reason is
mechanical, not stylistic: r3_rename_registry_occupancy_routing adds its registry read into the
model's PREDICTION through a per-dimension (diagonal, zero-init) scale while its aux loss trains
that read toward the RAW observation token. A diagonal scale cannot absorb a rotation, so that
head and any non-diagonal target map pull the same tensor in two different bases. The parent's
head and arch have no such coupling (r23's pointer injection is a full learnable (D,D) readout,
which can absorb any fixed linear map), and the parent's objective is exactly equivariant to a
linear target map: its FWL nuisance is a kernel-ridge solve that is linear in the target, so
crossfit(basis, T F^T) = crossfit(basis, T) F^T, and every one of its scales is a fraction of the
batch's own mean pair distance. Composing my map with the parent's objective therefore changes
the geometry it contrasts in without changing anything else about it.

WHAT Cov_contrast IS, MECHANICALLY
Inside make_target, under no_grad, with no trainable parameters:
  - Rows arriving in one call are the cmd-position rows of one batch, grouped by sequence in
    order. A sequence's first row is the one whose z_prev is exactly the zero vector (the harness
    builds z_prev as a strict-causal shift with a zero first column), so cumsum over those flags
    recovers a segment id per row without reading any command, path or step index.
  - Within a segment I form all pairwise squared distances, drop pairs closer than dup_frac times
    the mean within-segment pair distance (this is what removes the exact repeats: every mv /
    redirect step in this data renders to the same "cwd=... exit=0" observation, and a file is
    frequently cat'ed twice), take each row's nearest surviving neighbour, and keep only the rows
    in the closer half of that nearest-neighbour distribution.
  - Cov_contrast is the running second moment of those kept differences (z_i - z_nn(i)).
Cov_total is the running covariance of the same target rows.

WHY THIS SHOULD MOVE comp_ca AND NOT SOMETHING ELSE
The scored quantity is an N-way forced-foil pick: after a silent chain of moves, is the model's
predicted next observation closer to the content the chain actually routed to the read location
than to the other contents exposed in the same window. Whether that pick is right depends ONLY on
the projection of the prediction error onto the differences between the co-window candidates. Let
pred = true + e and let D = true - foil. The pick is correct iff 2 e.D + |D|^2 > 0. Decompose in a
basis where the candidate-difference second moment has eigenvalues b_i: the risk is governed by
sum_i b_i e_i^2, and an error component along a direction where all candidates agree (b_i = 0)
adds the SAME amount to every candidate's distance and cancels out of the comparison entirely.
So the accuracy the model should spend its capacity on is weighted by b_i -- and b_i is exactly
what Cov_contrast estimates, because the co-window exposed contents are within-sequence rows that
are each other's nearest non-duplicate neighbours. Training under F makes the loss a Mahalanobis
form whose per-direction weight is F^T F, i.e. approximately b^q s^(-a); with q = 1 that is the
weighting the argument above asks for, softened by a = 0.5 of the partner's whitening because the
model's achievable error in a direction also grows with that direction's own spread.

In the raw space the parent trains in, that weighting is badly wrong for this measurement: a
mean-pooled sentence embedding of a config file is dominated by "this is a config file", and the
canary that distinguishes one exposed content from its neighbour is a small, near-orthogonal
perturbation. Every unit of MSE the model spends getting the shared style right is invisible to
comp_ca. Concretely for the arch in this genome (r23 transports a content vector to a destination
address and then copies one raw prefix observation into the prediction), the gradient that says
"you copied the WRONG prefix observation" lives entirely in the difference directions this map
amplifies, and the gradient that says "you got the average file-shape right" lives in the
directions it damps.

WHY IT IS NOT THE PRIOR within_sequence_contrast_equalizer
That mechanism reweights inside a rank-32 subspace obtained by orthogonal iteration on the TOTAL
second moment, i.e. inside the 32 highest-energy directions -- which is where the shared style
lives and where the content differences are least likely to lie -- and it measures within-sequence
deviation from the sequence MEAN, which is dominated by ls-vs-cat-vs-empty structural contrast
rather than content-vs-content contrast. This map is full rank and is estimated from
nearest-neighbour PAIR DIFFERENCES, in which the common mode cancels by construction.

WHERE IT IS MEASURED, AND THE SWITCHED-OFF-AT-SCORING TRAP
Nothing in the applied map is conditioned on an observation being present. make_target is called
only during training. to_obs multiplies by a frozen buffer and adds a frozen center; it reads
neither z_prev nor any observation, so it behaves identically at the scored position whose own
observation is withheld. No resolved routing enters the model's input: the model's inputs are the
same tokens the parent gets, and the only thing that changes is the basis its training loss
measures error in.

CORRECTNESS OF THE INVERSE
Each factor is built from one eigendecomposition (float64, CPU) as V diag(g) V^T and
V diag(1/g) V^T from the SAME eigenpairs, so F F^-1 = C W W^-1 C^-1 = I algebraically; the global
scale is applied to F and its reciprocal to F^-1. Gains are log-centred (each factor is volume
preserving) and clamped to a factor of 3 either way, with shrinkage 0.05 toward the identity and
an eigenvalue floor, so the stored matrices are well conditioned and the float32 round trip is far
below the model's own error. The map is the identity until call 300, is rebuilt every 150 calls
with both exponents ramped linearly to full over 900 calls, and is frozen at call 1500, so the
majority of training happens against a fixed target geometry. Cost: one [n,768]x[768,768] matmul
per step, an [n,n] distance matrix on at most 640 rows every second step until the freeze, and
about nine pairs of 768x768 float64 eigendecompositions in total. All of this is unmeasured -- I
cannot run the harness from here -- but it is bounded by construction.

WHAT WOULD HAVE TO BE TRUE FOR THIS TO FAIL
1. If the nearest non-duplicate within-sequence neighbour is usually NOT another exposed content
   (e.g. it is an ls listing or a short error), Cov_contrast estimates the wrong difference
   subspace and the reweighting is noise. The closer-half filter is the defence; it is a
   heuristic, not a guarantee.
2. If the frozen encoder's low-energy directions are numerical junk rather than content, the
   a = 0.5 whitening factor amplifies junk. The shrinkage, the eigenvalue floor and the 3x gain
   cap bound how far, but do not eliminate it.
3. The parent objective recomputes a per-dimension precision weight (clamped to [0.25, 4]) in
   whatever space the target arrives in, so it partially re-flattens the DIAGONAL of my
   reweighting each batch. It cannot undo the rotation, but it does mean the realised weighting is
   milder than F^T F alone.
4. If comp_ca is in fact limited by the model never forming a routing computation at all -- rather
   than by forming one and then losing the read in a badly weighted metric -- then reshaping the
   metric changes the loss surface without changing what the model represents, and the number
   moves by noise.
