WHAT THE TWO PARENTS DO, AND WHAT I KEPT
----------------------------------------
Parent (r2_routed_collision_depth_batcher) replays each training sequence's commands through a
symbolic tracker to label every read with the number of hops that routed its content there, then
builds collision buckets keyed by (image, normalized read path, hop count), picking buckets with a
Boltzmann tilt toward deep hop counts and members with a tilt toward sequences that have many
routed reads. Kept verbatim in spirit: the symbolic replay, the exp(depth_beta * depth) bucket
tilt, the size^size_pow bucket tilt, the (1 + routed-read demand) member tilt, and the annealed
hard fraction.

Crossover partner (r6_sysblock_hardneg_curriculum) anneals batch composition from uniform toward
whole-system blocks — a small number of images per batch — to densify same-system in-batch
negatives. Kept verbatim as a block source: with probability img_share a block is drawn by
choosing n_block_images images size-weighted without replacement and sampling the block from their
union, with the same annealed hard fraction. The partner's tuned values (hard_frac_max 0.75,
n_block_images 1, ramp_frac 0.3) are carried over.

WHAT IS NEW — THE MECHANISM NEITHER PARENT COMPUTES
---------------------------------------------------
Both parents key their buckets on quantities that are IDENTICAL in the two arms of the scored
difference. The metric is native_hit - swap_stayed over the same board: the role-swapped arm keeps
the board, the destinations, the depth and the read location, and exchanges only WHICH CONTENT
plays which part in the move chain. A bucket keyed on (image, read path, hop count), as the parent
uses, is therefore a set of sequences that agree on the swap-invariant part and are unconstrained
— and typically homogeneous — on the part the swap actually permutes. Nothing in either parent
separates those two halves.

This impl separates them explicitly. The tracker is extended from hop COUNTING to content
IDENTITY: each read of a path no thread occupies starts a content thread; each mv / cp / redirect
carries the thread sitting at the source to the destination and increments its hop count; the set
of directories a thread touches is unioned so one trajectory (which contains several independent
groups — a cups mv chain plus several cvar accumulator groups) splits into connected windows. For
the sequence's deepest window the tracker emits two DISJOINT keys:

  BOARD = (number of threads that move, number of moves in the window, the window's maximum hop
           count, (digit-normalized 3-component root of the read path, its component count))
  ROLE  = (the read thread's OWN hop count, its birth rank among the moving threads, and a 0/1
           flag for whether it is the thread that made the window's opening move)

BOARD holds what a role swap leaves fixed; ROLE holds what a role swap permutes. Buckets are keyed
by BOARD alone, and a block draws its members ROUND-ROBIN over the DISTINCT ROLE keys present in
that bucket, tilting only inside a role by the parent's demand weight. The result is that one
batch contains, over a fixed board geometry, sequences in which the tested content is the deep one
and sequences in which it is the shallow one; sequences in which it was exposed first and in which
it was exposed second; sequences in which it made the opening move of the chain and in which it
did not.

WHY THIS SHOULD MOVE THE MEASURED QUANTITY
------------------------------------------
The objective in this genome (r22_exact_target_equivalence_quotient) is a listwise in-batch loss:
every step of every sequence in the batch is a row and every other row's target is a candidate.
Under board-matched, role-differing membership, the read row of sequence A must place A's content
nearer than the content of a board-matched sequence B whose read command has the same normalized
path shape and whose window has the same move count, same depth and same number of movers. A model
that answers from the read LOCATION cannot separate those rows at all. A model that answers from a
position-in-the-chain rule — "the content that moved first", "the content exposed first", "the
deeper content" — is contradicted inside the batch, because the block deliberately contains rows
where that rule points at a different content. The only in-batch-separating feature left is which
content the chain actually carried to the read location. That is exactly the quantity the paired
difference measures: the location-keyed predictor contributes structurally zero to comp_ca, and
the position-keyed predictor is the analytic non-tracker band the run reports beside the score.
Training pressure that specifically penalizes both of those, while leaving content tracking as the
only way to reduce the loss, is why I expect a step change rather than a nudge.

A second, smaller change: the uniform remainder of every batch is drawn WITHOUT replacement from a
reshuffled permutation (random reshuffling) rather than iid torch.randint. Under a fixed step
budget this gives exact epoch coverage and lower sampling variance than with-replacement SGD;
both parents draw iid.

MEASURED HERE, AND WHAT IS NOT MEASURED
---------------------------------------
Checked against evolve/jail_data/train_sample.jsonl (24 real training trajectories): the tracker's
recovered move count and window depth agree with the withheld generator metadata on every one of
the 24 (e.g. sequence 3 -> 8 moves, depth 5, 2 movers vs. the file's R=8, depth=5, two movers;
sequence 22 -> 8 moves, depth 4, 3 movers vs. R=8, depth=4, three movers). The batcher's contract
(exactly bs indices, in range, deterministic given seed, seed-sensitive, no global RNG, fit never
mutated) was exercised with a stand-in RNG at bs in {8, 64, 960, 1000}. Batch-composition
enrichment was measured only on an artificially replicated corpus, where in-batch same-board pair
mass roughly doubled over uniform; that number is not a claim about the real split, whose board
diversity is much higher and where the enrichment should be larger. NOTHING about the effect on
comp_ca has been measured — no training was run in this jail.

WHERE IT IS AND IS NOT ACTIVE
-----------------------------
This axis runs only inside the training loop, choosing which sequence indices to train on. It puts
nothing into the model's input: the model still sees exactly the interleaved (cmd, obs) embeddings
baseline_interleave builds, and the resolved routing computed here never reaches a token, a target
or a scoring path. The tracker reads a sequence's COMMAND STRINGS only — never an observation, an
embedding, or an `ok` flag — so it is not gated on an observation being present and cannot be
switched off at a position whose observation is withheld. It also never runs at scoring time.

WHAT WOULD HAVE TO BE TRUE FOR THIS TO FAIL
-------------------------------------------
1. Board buckets are too sparse. If the real training split has so many distinct BOARD keys that
   few buckets hold twelve members, blocks degrade toward single-role picks and the impl collapses
   to something close to the parent. The board key was deliberately coarsened (movers, move count,
   max depth, path root/arity — not the depth multiset, not the literal file name) to avoid this,
   but I could not count real buckets from inside the jail.
2. Board matching is not the binding constraint. If the r22 listwise loss is already saturated by
   the ~1900 rows a batch produces, adding a few dozen genuinely confusable candidates may not
   change the gradient enough to matter.
3. Concentration hurts. group_size=12 with hard_frac_max=0.75 puts roughly three boards across
   thirty-six of sixty-four sequences late in training. If the arch and the head need broad
   trajectory diversity per step more than they need hard board-matched negatives, this trades
   away more than it buys; the annealed ramp only protects the opening 30% of training.
4. The role axes are the wrong ones. If the model's shortcut is neither first-mover, nor
   exposure order, nor own-depth — some other position statistic — the round-robin does not
   contradict it and the contrast is wasted effort.
5. The tracker mislabels. Trajectories whose windows interleave through a shared directory would
   be merged by the union-find into one window, inflating the move count and blurring the board.
   On the 24 real samples the cups window and each cvar group separated cleanly, but that is 24
   sequences from a training split I cannot see.
