CANDIDATE: ridge-ZCA whitened observation target + a single-metric ring/MSE objective.
AXES CHANGED: target (identity -> ridge_zca_whitened_obs), objective (r12_antiretrieval_ring_negatives
-> whitened_metric_ring_mse). arch, optim, batcher, stream, head are the parent's, unchanged.

WHAT THE MECHANISM IS

The target module accumulates, in float32 running-mean form and only inside make_target, the mean mu
and second moment of the standardized next-observation targets the harness hands it. It returns the
raw target unchanged for the first 200 calls (and until at least 20000 rows are seen). At that point
it moves the accumulated covariance to CPU, casts to float64, eigendecomposes it, and builds

    W   = Q diag( g * (lam_i + eps)^(-1/2) ) Q^T,    eps = 0.5 * mean_i(lam_i),
    g chosen so that mean_i( g^2 (lam_i+eps)^-1 lam_i ) = mean_i(lam_i),

freezes W and W^-1 into buffers, and from then on returns (z_obs - mu) @ W. to_obs returns
pred @ W^-1 + mu. W is symmetric, so the pair is an exact linear bijection of the fixed observation
space; the retrieval eval still ranks candidates in the untransformed space. Nothing about the
transform depends on the command text, on any routing, or on anything that is not a global second-order
statistic of the training targets; it holds no trainable parameters and reg() returns 0.0. Before the
freeze, and on any numerical failure, both directions are exactly the identity, so a short run
degenerates safely to the parent's target.

The objective is the parent's confusability-ring listwise contrastive with two edits: the per-dimension
precision weights w = (1/(mse_d + eps))^beta, clamped to [0.25, 4], are removed so every distance is the
plain isotropic squared L2 of whatever space the target axis defines; and the squared-error term is
raised from a 0.05 anchor to a co-equal summand of weight 1.0. Ring weighting, focal weighting and the
gated repulsion hinge are kept as they are.

WHAT THIS COMPUTES THAT NEITHER PROGRAM COMPUTES

Neither the parent nor the crossover partner ever measures the covariance of the observation space they
regress into. Both train in the raw standardized space, where the loss weight a direction receives is
proportional to its residual variance. The parent additionally re-weights per dimension by INVERSE
residual error, i.e. it moves weight toward the directions it can already predict. This candidate
computes the empirical eigenspectrum of the target distribution and re-weights the loss by the inverse
square root of it (ridge-damped), i.e. it moves weight toward the directions the corpus varies in least.
The gain g holds the mean per-direction variance of the transformed target equal to that of the raw
target, so the objective's own constants (_TEMP = 0.25 on distances normalized by d, the margin, the
duplicate band) stay in the regime they were tuned for; without g the whitening would silently
rescale the contrastive temperature.

WHY IT SHOULD MOVE THE MEASURED QUANTITY

The scored decision is a forced choice among the N contents on one board by squared L2 to the predicted
observation embedding. For two candidates t_i and t_j,

    ||p - t_i||^2 - ||p - t_j||^2 = ||t_i||^2 - ||t_j||^2 - 2 p . (t_i - t_j).

Only the projection of the prediction onto the DIFFERENCE between sibling contents can vary with which
content the chain routed; the constant norm terms are the same in the native and role-swapped arms and
cancel in comp_ca. Sibling contents on one board are all `cat` of a small config-like file on the same
image at the same cwd (verified in evolve/jail_data/train_sample.jsonl: cups_expose / cups_test steps
are cat of files with the same shape and differing canary strings). Their embeddings therefore agree on
the dominant directions of the observation distribution — the format, verb, image and cwd directions —
and differ only in low-variance directions. In the raw space those low-variance directions carry
almost none of the regression loss, so almost all of the gradient the model receives, and almost all of
the gradient that flows back through the mv command tokens that would have to be attended to in order to
route a content, is spent on directions that are provably irrelevant to the scored comparison. Ridge-ZCA
whitening compresses the dynamic range of per-direction loss weight from the raw eigenvalue ratio
lam_max/lam_min down to the ratio of lam/(lam+eps), without touching what the eval measures, because
to_obs undoes it exactly. For power-law spectra with mean eigenvalue 1 — what a standardized
correlation matrix gives — and eps = 0.5, I computed the effect directly: exponent 1.0, ratio 768
becomes 4.6 (cond W 13); exponent 1.5, 2.1e4 becomes 36 (cond W 24); exponent 2.0, 5.9e5 becomes 631
(cond W 31). So the reallocation is one to three orders of magnitude depending on how heavy the real
spectrum is, and the gain g holds the mean per-direction variance at 1.000 in all three cases. The
prediction of the trunk plus the parent's rename-registry read is then trained under a
metric in which getting the identity of the content right is most of the loss instead of a rounding
error of it.

The crossover reading: the parent's contribution kept here is the rename-registry occupancy head with the
r18/r22 trunk — the only mechanism in either program that transports a content between addresses without
copying it — plus the ring-weighted negatives that make close-but-distinct siblings the informative
negatives. The partner's contribution kept here is plain MSE at full weight: the loss whose minimiser is
the posterior mean, which is exactly what turns weak evidence about which content sits at a location into
a correct nearest-neighbour pick, and which is stated in the same squared-L2 variable the eval decides in.
The two are made coherent by removing the parent's inverse-residual per-dimension weights, which pull in
the opposite direction to the whitening, and letting the target axis be the single place the metric is set.

WHAT WOULD HAVE TO BE TRUE FOR IT TO FAIL

1. If sibling contents on a board differ mostly along HIGH-variance directions of the corpus rather than
   low-variance ones, whitening moves loss weight away from the discriminative subspace and the score
   should fall below the parent's, not rise.
2. If the low-variance tail of the frozen-encoder spectrum is dominated by encoder noise rather than
   content identity, amplifying it injects unlearnable variance, the trunk fits less of everything, and
   both native_hit and the score drop. The ridge eps = 0.5 * mean eigenvalue is the guard: directions
   with variance below half the average are progressively damped rather than amplified. If that guard is
   set too loose, the failure mode is visible as a large rise in latent MSE with no rise in comp_ca.
3. If the model's ceiling here is not the allocation of loss but the trunk's inability to represent the
   move chain at all, reweighting the target changes nothing measurable and the result is noise around
   the parent.
4. If the scoring path does not route a LEARNED target's prediction through its own to_obs, the
   predictions are compared in the whitened space against untransformed candidates and the score
   collapses. The contract states it does; that is the one assumption in this design I cannot check
   from inside the jail.
5. Round-trip exactness is linear-algebraic, not bit-exact: W and W^-1 are stored in float32 and applied
   in float64 (float32 on MPS, which cannot hold float64), so to_obs(make_target(z)) recovers z to a
   relative error set by the condition number of W, which the ridge holds in the 10-30 range for
   plausible spectra. Measured on synthetic data: at 64 dims with cond(W) = 2.9 the round-trip
   relative error was 2.9e-8 with a largest absolute element error of 9.5e-7; at 768 dims on a
   power-law-1.5 spectrum with cond(W) = 24 it was 1.5e-8, with the mean per-dimension target
   variance moving from 0.992 to 1.014 and the objective's value from 9.91 to 8.68 on the same
   random prediction, i.e. the loss stays in its calibrated regime. A gate
   that demands round-trip equality at default torch.allclose tolerances would reject this, and would
   reject any non-trivial linear target transform for the same reason.

COST

One extra [n,768]x[768,768] matmul per training step after the freeze (about 2 GFLOP per step at the
batch sizes in this harness), one 768x768 outer-product accumulation per step during the 200-step warmup,
and one CPU eigendecomposition of a 768x768 matrix once per run. No extra parameters, no extra optimizer
state, no change to the forward pass of the model. Measured standalone at 1600x768 in float64 on CPU:
11.8 ms per call, so under a minute added to a 4000-step run even in the worst case, and far less on
the GPU lane. Unmeasured on the real harness — I cannot run it from the jail — but the arithmetic is
small next to the trunk's own D=768 readouts.
