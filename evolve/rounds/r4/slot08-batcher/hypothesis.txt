CANDIDATE: batcher = r4_hopdepth_scaffold_stratified_quota
AXES CHANGED: batcher only. Every other axis is inherited from the parent unchanged.

WHAT THE CROSSOVER KEEPS FROM EACH PROGRAM

From the parent's batcher (r6_sysblock_hardneg_curriculum) I keep image blocking: the non-uniform
part of a batch is restricted to a small number of system images sampled per batch (default 1, the
parent's tuned setting), so the negatives the objective sees inside a batch come from the same
system rather than from a mix of distributions.

From the partner's batcher (r2_routed_collision_depth_batcher) I keep the symbolic content tracker:
each sequence's command list is replayed to find, for every read, how many hops of routing put the
content there. My tracker is written fresh but obeys the same physics: `mv A B` retires A and gives
B the content one hop further; `cp A B` and `cat A > B` give B the content one hop further while A
keeps its own; `cat A >> B` takes max(hops(B), hops(A)+1); a bare `cat/head/tail A` reports the hop
count currently standing at A. I dropped the partner's path-shape normalization: bucket identity
plays no role in my design, only the hop number does.

WHAT I ADD THAT NEITHER PARENT COMPUTES

Both parents treat depth as a static tilt realized by i.i.d. sampling: they anneal only HOW MUCH of
each batch is drawn from the non-uniform pool, never WHICH part of the hop scale that pool sits on,
and both leave the realized depth content of any individual batch to chance. Mine computes two
things neither computes:

1. A MOVING FRONTIER OVER THE HOP SCALE. f(step) walks linearly from 1 hop to d_top (the deepest
   stratum holding at least min_stratum sequences) over the opening ramp_frac of training. Stratum
   shares come from an asymmetric kernel around f: exp(-(d-f)/tau_up) above the frontier (sharp,
   so chains deeper than the current frontier are held back) and exp(-(f-d)*lam_down) below it
   (mild, so every already-passed depth keeps a permanent share instead of being displaced). The
   mixture is therefore ORDERED easy-to-hard and CUMULATIVE, not merely more concentrated over
   time.

2. AN EXACT PER-BATCH DEPTH QUOTA. The shares are turned into integer counts by largest-remainder
   allocation and each stratum's count is drawn from that stratum, so every single batch carries
   the scheduled depth spectrum. This is stratified allocation replacing i.i.d. sampling: it does
   not change the intended mixture, it removes the between-batch variance of the mixture. A count
   is capped at its stratum's size and the freed slots go to the uniform part, so a thin stratum
   is never resampled to fill a large quota.

The remaining (1 - curr_frac) of every batch stays uniform over the whole split so the rest of the
pack keeps training and the composed blend does not collapse onto routing sequences.

WHY THIS SHOULD MOVE THE MEASURED QUANTITY

comp_ca is a paired difference on windows of hop depth 3 and deeper: to score, the model must
follow a chain of at least three silent moves and return the content the NATIVE chain routed there,
and must stop returning it when the same board is re-chained so that a different content lands
there. Resolving a k-hop chain reuses the (k-1)-hop resolution as a subroutine, and training is a
fixed step budget on frozen embeddings, so what matters is not only how much deep-chain gradient
arrives but whether the shallow-hop copy is already reliable when the deep chains start to dominate
the update. That ordering is exactly what neither parent controls.

The second effect is variance. Deep windows are the rare and informative part of the signal: in the
real trajectories in this jail one sequence carries one cups window plus a few shallow cvar groups,
so a whole sequence contributes a single deep routed read. Under i.i.d. block sampling a sizeable
share of batches carry almost no deep window at all, and those updates move the shared transition
machinery without any deep-chain constraint on it. A guaranteed quota removes those batches.
Measured on the jail's 24 real training trajectories replicated to 1920 sequences: uniform sampling
gives 50% of a batch at depth>=3 and 4% at the deepest stratum; after the ramp my batches hold
about 78% at depth>=3 and 25% at the deepest stratum while still holding about 20% at depths 1-2.
That is a roughly six-fold enrichment of the deepest stratum with the shallow scaffold retained.
No claim is made here about the resulting score; it is unmeasured.

WHAT HAD TO BE TRUE, AND WHAT WOULD MAKE IT FAIL

The hop labels must be right. I checked the tracker against the recorded ground truth in the jail's
sample: on all 24 sequences the hop count it computes for the window's test read equals the depth
recorded in that step's metadata (24/24, no mismatches). If the real split's command mix contains
routing forms the parser does not model, the strata become noise and the design degrades toward a
noisier version of the parents.

It fails if the routing circuit is not order-sensitive - if a model that sees deep chains from step
one learns them just as well. Then the early phase has spent its steps on the shallowest stratum
for nothing, and the candidate should land below both parents rather than between them.

It fails if the deep strata in the real split are small. The quota then repeatedly draws the same
few sequences within one image block, and because the eval holds out images, memorizing them buys
nothing. The stratum-size cap and the uniform remainder bound this but do not remove it; a smaller
curr_frac or a larger min_stratum is the lever.

It fails if the ~80% of reads that are not routed at all dominate the loss whatever the sequence
mixture is - the batcher can choose sequences but not steps, so its leverage on the per-step
gradient mix is bounded by how much of a sequence is routing.

WHY NOT MORE AXES

The partner's arch, head and objective are co-adapted with each other and I cannot run the harness
to check an interaction, so importing one of them would put the result at the mercy of an untested
pairing. The partner's contribution that belongs on my axis - the symbolic hop tracker - is the one
I took.

COST

Setup replays every sequence's commands once: 0.03 s for 1920 sequences here, linear in total
commands. Steady state is a handful of small multinomial draws per step: 0.19 s for 4000 steps at
bs=64. Determinism, bounds, exact length, and non-mutation of fit were exercised over the real
sample and over degenerate inputs (bs=1, bs>n, n=1, single image, empty command lists,
unparseable commands, no routed read anywhere, curr_frac=0).
