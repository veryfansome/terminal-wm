CANDIDATE: arch = r24_backchain_pointer_resolution (crossover; only the arch axis changes)

WHAT THE TWO PROGRAMS CONTRIBUTE

Parent: the r18 path-state latent-transition trunk (arch) and the dual-address move-transport head
(head) — a source/destination decomposition of each command plus a contrastive aux that mines
steps whose observation duplicates an earlier observation under a different command with a
content-free step in between, i.e. exactly the reads that follow a move chain.
Partner: r22's prefix-content cross-attention (arch) — a masked softmax over strictly-earlier
steps whose VALUES are the raw 768-d observation embeddings, so the prediction can be an exact
copy of an observation the model has already seen.

My arch subclasses R22PrefixContentXAttention, so both the r18 trunk and the r22 prefix-copy
channel survive untouched; the genome keeps the parent's head, so the composed-read contrastive
aux survives too. The partner's head (transition forward-model consistency) is the one thing I
drop: only one head fits, and the parent's is the one whose mined examples land on the scored kind
of position. What I add is a third channel that neither program has.

WHAT I ADD

A K-hop BACKWARD POINTER CHASE over the command sequence, computed at every command position.

1. Addresses. Each command's raw frozen 768-d embedding x_i goes through LayerNorm, one shared
   GELU layer -> a_i in R^256, then two learned DIAGONAL role gates sigmoid(role_src),
   sigmoid(role_dst) in R^256, and then ONE SHARED projection addr_proj: R^256 -> R^64, unit-
   normalized. So src_addr_i and dst_addr_i are two role-selected views of the same feature vector
   pushed through the SAME map: "first path in this command" and "second path in this command",
   living in one common address space by construction. Both parents (and r2's head) instead use two
   independent linear heads, which have no structural reason to place the same path string at the
   same address when it appears as the destination of one mv and as the source of the next.
   Addresses depend on that command alone — not on the trunk hidden state, not on context.

2. Follow matrix. follow[j, j'] = softmax over j' < j of
   <src_addr_j, dst_addr_j'> / temp - softplus(recency) * (j - j' - 1), masked to valid earlier
   commands. Read as: standing at step j, jump back to the most recent earlier step that PUT
   something at the location step j is asking about. temp and recency are learned scalars.

3. Absorb probability. absorb_j = sigmoid(MLP(a_j, obs_feat(obs_j))) * (obs_j is live). Read as:
   does step j's own observation carry the content, or must the chase keep going? This is the
   mechanism the data demands: in the training trajectories every `mv A B` step has empty output,
   so its rendered observation is the constant "cwd=... exit=0\n" and carries no content, while a
   `cat P` step's observation IS the content. A move step must be a pass-through node, not an
   answer.

4. Chase. alpha_1 = follow[i, :]; alpha_{t+1} = (alpha_t * (1 - absorb)) @ follow;
   acc = sum_t alpha_t * absorb. Mass only ever moves to strictly earlier positions, so after K=8
   hops acc[i, j] is the probability that a chain of address matches starting at command i is
   absorbed at earlier step j. copy_i = (acc_i @ obs) / max(mass_i, 1e-4) — a convex combination of
   raw earlier observation embeddings — is injected into pred at command position i through a
   (D,D) readout initialized to 0.05*I (the answer is a COPY of an observation, so identity is the
   right prior; the spectral cap in the genome's optim keeps that readout bounded) under a sigmoid
   gate fed the absorbed mass, the chase sharpness and the copy's RMS.

WHAT THIS COMPUTES THAT NEITHER PROGRAM COMPUTES

A resolution of the move chain by COMPOSITION AT READ TIME, backwards. The parent's memories
(r18's path slots, r2's head transport) compose forwards and incrementally: content is physically
carried through an outer-product store one write at a time, so per-hop addressing error multiplies
over depth and there is no place to re-sharpen. The partner's cross-attention is exactly ONE hop:
it can copy an earlier observation but cannot ask "and where did the thing at THAT location come
from". Mine composes several address matches into one retrieval, each hop a normalized softmax, and
it is the composition itself that is differentiable: the gradient of the copied content flows back
through every hop's address match, so the training signal that says "you copied the wrong content"
lands on the addressing at every link of the chain rather than only on the last write.

WHY THIS SHOULD MOVE comp_ca

comp_ca is (native_hit - swap_stayed) over windows where a content is routed by a chain of silent
moves and one location is then read. In these trajectories the observation of `cat <final path>` is
the byte-identical text of the earlier `cat <original path>` (same cwd, same exit, same file bytes),
so its frozen embedding is the SAME vector: a correct chase makes the prediction an exact copy of a
vector that is in the candidate set, which is as good as a prediction can be under the nearest-
candidate metric. Under the role-swapped arm the move commands are re-encoded, so the follow matrix
changes, the chase lands on the partner's content, and swap_stayed goes to 0 while native_hit stays
1 — the per-window difference is +1. Keying on the read location alone cannot do this: the read
command is identical in both arms, so any channel that only looks at it contributes exactly 0.
I verified the algebra with an oracle-addressed forward pass: on a depth-3 chain the chase returns
the moved content with cosine 1.000, and on the role-swapped version of the same board it returns
the partner's content instead.

WHAT WOULD HAVE TO BE TRUE FOR IT TO FAIL

- The pooled frozen embedding of "mv A B" may not linearly separate A from B. Both paths are mean-
  pooled into one vector; if the encoder's contextual token embeddings do not keep enough of the
  first-vs-second-path distinction, the role gates cannot recover two different addresses and the
  follow matrix degenerates. This is the single biggest risk, and it is the same bet the parent's
  dual-address head already makes — I make it with a stronger structural prior (shared projection,
  role selection by gating) rather than a weaker one (two free linear heads).
- Learning the chase end-to-end may be too slow for the step budget. Nothing supervises the hops
  directly; they get gradient from the genome's contrastive objective and from the head's aux on
  the final prediction. Mitigations built in: the readout starts at 0.05*I rather than 0 so the
  addressing has first-order gradient from step 1, and absorb starts near 0.5 everywhere so all
  hops carry mass at init.
- If absorb learns to be ~1 everywhere, the chase collapses to a single hop and the channel becomes
  a second copy of the partner's prefix cross-attention with raw-command addresses. That is a
  graceful degradation, not a break, but then the candidate should score like the partner, not
  above it.
- The recency bias could dominate: if softplus(recency) stays large, the chase walks back one step
  at a time regardless of address. It is initialized to ~0.25 per intervening step against an
  address score range of +/-2 (temp 0.5, learned) and is itself learnable.

WHAT IT IS NOT

No command text is parsed anywhere; nothing in this file knows what "mv" is. The addresses, the
follow matrix, the absorb decision and the number of effective hops are all learned from the frozen
embeddings. Nothing resolved by my code is placed in the model's input: the chase runs inside the
module on the same tokens the harness hands it, and it only ever reads strictly earlier positions.

CORRECTNESS CHECKED HERE

- forward is bit-comparable to r22 at init up to the 0.05*I readout (max |delta| 0.028).
- Causality: perturbing the observation at pair j moves no command prediction at index <= j
  (max delta exactly 0.0 at every tested j) and does move later ones.
- L in {0,1,2,3,4,5,7,33}, padded rows, and the odd-L case where the last command has no
  observation: finite outputs, correct shapes.
- Six real training steps with the genome's objective, optim (Muon + spectral cap) and head: finite
  losses, gradient reaching every new parameter.
- Cost: +8% wall clock per fwd+bwd over r22 at B=64, L=64 on CPU (the chase is K-1 batched
  [n,n]x[n,n] matmuls).
